## Description

Client needs a clone of this:

[https://theredpen.sups.ai/](https://theredpen.sups.ai/ "smartCard-inline")


Essay Brainstorming: Redpen allows students to input information about themselves, their college list, extracurricular activities, background, etc. It then uses this data to generate initial essay topic ideas and brainstorming prompts.

Essay Draft Generation: Based on the student's inputted information, Redpen can produce the initial draft of the college essay. However, Alex noted this draft would likely need significant polishing, as the AI-generated content may be too generic.

College List Integration: Redpen integrates the student's college list into the essay writing process, tailoring the brainstorming and draft generation to the specific schools the student is applying to.

Centralized Platform: Redpen provides a single platform where students can manage their college application essays, from ideation to initial drafting.

Lead Generation Potential: Alex saw value in Redpen as a lead generation tool for Admissions Angle, as it could attract students to their services by providing an engaging essay writing assistant.

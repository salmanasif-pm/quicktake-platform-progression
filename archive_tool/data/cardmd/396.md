## Description

- **Business Goal:**
  - AI powered software that understands business operations and challenges to suggest possible solutions.
- **Idea:**
  - Learning Language Model will be custom trained on pre-defined Datasets that will interact with users, ask questions and draft a solution doc.
- **Business Model:**
  - SaaS (Suggestion needed on Web App vs Extension)
- **Pricing:**
  - Not sure. Could be Module based with Limited Free accessibility.
- **User Roles:**
  - Super Admin
  - Enterprise/Franchisee Owner (Admin)
  - Franchisee (Manager)
  - Writer
  - Reader
- **Business Flow:**
  - Enterprise generates new location for a Franchise
  - Franchisee go to website
  - Login
  - Gamify in Writing about your business
  - AI Generates questions about business processes
  - Includes problem questions
  - Answers gets put in to the Core Database
  - Feedback loop for info if something is missing within Writer & Reader
  - Once the Information is Completed
  - Draft a Document as the outcome for the operations.
- **Look & Feel:**
  - A well spaced document with ability to add their logos and use Font colors as per the brands design guide.

## Description

### Problem Statement

The core issue centers around inefficiencies in connecting seniors to suitable care facilities, balancing complex datasets, HIPAA compliance, and optimizing customer experience. The current manual processes for gathering information, evaluating care needs, and matching seniors with appropriate facilities are time-consuming and labor-intensive. This impacts both the business's scalability and the quality of service provided to seniors and their families.
So, the prospect wants to automate the process to decrease the time to touch for his process to increase efficiency.

**Key Requirements (**Process Automation**)**

1. **Automated Data Collection**:
   - Implement **speech-to-text transcription** to extract actionable data from family/patient conversations.
   - Develop a **form builder tool** that collects static and dynamic patient data, including care plans, preferences, and financials.
   - Ensure that the data collection process complies with **HIPAA** and financial privacy regulations.
2. **Data Integration and Processing**:
   - Create a **centralized data repository** for managing patient profiles, facility information, and static data fields.
   - Develop an **AI algorithm** to match patient profiles with suitable facilities based on preferences, care needs, and financial constraints.
   - Enable cross-referencing of patient needs with **real-time facility availability** if available or prompt updates when occupancy changes.
3. **Communication Automation**:
   - Integrate with tools like **Twilio** for automated email and SMS communication with facility managers.
   - Automatically send patient profiles and care plans to relevant facilities, emphasizing alignment with their capabilities.
4. **Scheduling and Follow-Up**:
   - Include a **tour scheduling feature** to coordinate visits between families and facilities.
   - Automate follow-ups for updates on patient placement or additional care requirements.
5. **Reporting and Analytics**:
   - Generate detailed **reports** summarizing placement activity, success rates, and time-to-placement metrics.
   - Provide analytics to predict facility vacancies and identify trends in senior care needs.

### Current Business Operation Process Flow (Pre-Automation)

#### **1. Patient Referral and Initial Contact**

- **Input**:
  - Referral from a case manager, discharge nurse, or family member.
  - Communication via text, email, or phone call.
- **Action**:
  - Placement agent receives notification of a patient requiring placement, including basic details (e.g., discharge date, care plan).
  - Agent reaches out to the patient or family for additional information.

#### **2. Data Collection**

- **Input**:
  - Family/patient interview (phone or in-person).
  - Assessment form sent to families for detailed information (optional).
- **Action**:
  - Placement agent manually collects patient information such as:
    - Care requirements (e.g., skilled nursing, therapy needs).
    - Lifestyle preferences (e.g., amenities, dietary restrictions).
    - Financial constraints (budget and insurance details).
  - Data is entered into spreadsheets or other manual tools.

#### **3. Facility Search and Match**

- **Input**:
  - Patient profile created from collected data.
  - Static facility data stored in Word documents or spreadsheets.
- **Action**:
  - Placement agent manually filters facilities based on:
    - Location preferences.
    - Care plan alignment.
    - Lifestyle and amenity match.
  - Agent cross-references facility availability (e.g., open beds) by:
    - Sending broadcast emails to community managers.
    - Calling individual facilities to check occupancy and suitability.

#### **4. Communication with Facilities**

- **Input**:
  - Patient profile.
  - Selected facilities based on manual search.
- **Action**:
  - Placement agent prepares emails or calls facilities to share patient details.
  - Facility managers respond with availability and potential fit.
  - Scheduling tours or visits, if necessary.

#### **5. Patient Placement**

- **Input**:
  - Facility responses and availability confirmations.
  - Finalized patient preferences and care plan.
- **Action**:
  - Placement agent coordinates with the family to visit and select a facility.
  - Final placement decision is made.
  - Agent confirms placement with the facility and schedules move-in date.

#### **6. Post-Placement Follow-Up**

- **Input**:
  - Feedback from families and facilities.
  - Ongoing care updates (if required).
- **Action**:
  - Agent collects reviews and referrals.
  - Reviews process performance for improvement opportunities.

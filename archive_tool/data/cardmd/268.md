## Description

The prospect is looking to build a web based platform that users can visit without needing to download an app. It should work on computers and mobile browsers.

A friendly digital person (avatar) appears on the screen. The avatar looks human and talks back to the user. It should feel warm, helpful, and kind  like a guide during a tough time.

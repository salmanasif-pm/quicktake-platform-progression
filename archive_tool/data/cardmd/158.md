## Description

## Problem Statement

_What The Future Education_ is currently delivering impactful human-skills-focused learning programs, but their **existing technology infrastructure (WordPress + LearnPress + WooCommerce)** is **not scalable or robust enough** to support:

- Expected growth in users (hundreds per month, both B2B schools and B2C families).
- Diverse program formats (multi-module career orientation programs + short life-skills courses).
- Their **long-term vision** of building a gamified, scalable, multi-language learning platform accessible even in low-connectivity environments.

The lack of a tailored, purpose-built learning system limits their ability to:

- Efficiently serve schools and families at scale.
- Provide engaging, gamified experiences to learners.
- Offer teachers and administrators effective oversight tools.
- Establish themselves as the **“Duolingo of human skills”** with global reach.

---

## 2. Current Setup (As-Is)

- **Platform:** WordPress + LearnPress + WooCommerce.
- **Users:** B2B (schools) and B2C (families).
- **Challenges with current setup:**
  - Limited scalability.
  - Difficult to manage multiple user roles effectively.
  - Insufficient analytics and engagement tools.

---

## 3. Short-Term Direction

- **Migration to Thinkific** to gain stability and immediate scalability while planning for custom development.

---

## 4. Long-Term Solution (Custom LMS Requirements)

### A) **User Roles & Dashboards**

- **Students**
  - Personalized dashboard with course progress.
  - Access to structured modules (e.g., _Who am I?_, _Where do I want to go?_, _How do I get there?_).
  - Tools for self-reflection, SMART goal tracking, and **1/3/5-year action plans**.
  - Gamified progress indicators (badges, streaks, rewards).
- **Teachers**
  - Dashboard to monitor student progress, engagement, and completion rates.
  - Ability to review assignments and provide feedback.
  - Reporting tools for class-level insights.
- **Administrators**
  - Manage users (students, teachers, schools).
  - Manage courses and program structure.
  - Oversee both **B2B and B2C enrollments**.
  - Access to analytics on adoption and performance.

---

### B) **Learning Experience & Content Delivery**

- Support for **long programs** (multi-module career orientation) and **short “life courses”** (e.g., conflict management, decision-making).
- Structured, guided progression with clear milestones.
- Interactive self-assessments embedded in courses.
- Ability to integrate different content formats (video, text, activities, worksheets).

---

### C) **Gamification & Engagement**

- Strong emphasis on gamified learning:
  - Streaks, badges, levels, and progress milestones.
  - Engagement features modeled after **Duolingo** to encourage consistency.
- Designed to increase stickiness with both students and schools.

---

### D) **Accessibility & Performance**

- **Low internet consumption** → essential for public schools with limited connectivity.
- **Mobile-first design** with future potential for a dedicated mobile app.
- **Multi-language support** for international reach.
- **Scalable architecture** to accommodate growing adoption.

---

### E) **Administration & Analytics**

- Real-time analytics dashboards for teachers and admins.
- Reporting features for schools (B2B clients).
- Tools to measure impact of programs (student progress, engagement, and goal achievement).

---

## 5. Key Expectations for Solution

- An **MVP that is lightweight but scalable**.
- Expandable into a **full K-12 offering** over time.
- Positioned as an **innovative, gamified, human-skills-focused learning platform**.

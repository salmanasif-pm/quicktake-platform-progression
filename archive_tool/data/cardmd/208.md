## Description

Prospect needs to automate his workflow for his healthcare business.

I’ve added the recording and the requirements below for your reference.

#### **Integration Requirements**

1. **CRM to EMR Integration:**
   - Automate data transfer from CRM to EMR after client intake.
   - Ensure compatibility and explore third-party APIs or middleware if direct integration isn’t possible.
2. **Data Synchronization:**
   - Sync basic client details (name, phone number, email, symptoms) between systems.
   - Maintain a single source of truth to avoid discrepancies between platforms.
3. **Scheduling Integration:**
   - Use a scheduling assistant (e.g., Outlook, Google Calendar) to auto-schedule discovery calls and appointments.
   - Allow real-time updates based on staff availability.

#### **Automation Requirements**

1. **Forms and Intake:**
   - Implement a form that clients complete after clicking an ad, capturing essential details to populate the CRM automatically.
   - Trigger automated workflows based on the completed form.
2. **Post-Treatment Communication:**
   - Automate follow-up messages for clients who complete treatment (e.g., newsletters, boosters).
   - Integrate text and email communications through CRM workflows.
3. **Lead Management:**
   - Build pipelines in the CRM to manage client stages (e.g., discovery, active treatment, booster follow-ups).
   - Automate transitions between stages based on defined triggers.

#### **Error Reduction**

1. **Manual Data Entry Minimization:**
   - Auto-log incoming calls or texts into the CRM with client information.
   - Auto-fill forms to eliminate repetitive data entry.
2. **Data Validation:**
   - Validate client information during intake to ensure accuracy.

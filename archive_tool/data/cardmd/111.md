**Card Title:** Discovery Analysis: Stairway Invest (Austin Fowler)

**Description:**

**Client & Business Model Overview**

- **Client:** Austin Fowler (Stairway Invest)
- **Location:** US West Coast
- **Current Scale:** ~500 clients, 23 years in business.
- **Existing Tech Team:** Small internal team (1 Full-time, 2 Part-time).

**The Core Concept (Crucial Context)** Stairway acts as an investment education platform that offers "Savings Accounts" (legally defined as Regulation D, 506C/B personally guaranteed debt funds).

- **Value Prop:** They pay 8% APY (beating standard US banks).
- **Mechanism:** They operate on a fractional reserve model (keeping ~20% or the sum of the top 2 clients' funds in cash).
- **The Upsell:** They teach users how to invest, eventually helping them set up their _own_ debt funds (charging ~$15k in legal fees) to raise capital up to $150M.
- **Legal Note:** We cannot use the words "Bank" or "Deposit" in public copy due to SEC regulations.

**Current Platform Functionality (As-Is)**

- **User Roles:**
  1. **Investor:** Can deposit/withdraw funds (via Dwolla integration). Requires US Tax ID & Address.
  2. **Fundraiser:** Can create accounts for others, share calendar links, and raise capital.
- **Payment Gateway:** Dwolla (US Only).
- **Risk Management:** System enforces a maximum deposit limit per client to prevent "Whales" (over-concentration of risk).

**Pain Points & Scope of Work (To-Be)** Austin wants to scale from 500 clients to thousands. The current manual processes are the bottleneck.

1. **Transaction Automation (Priority)**
   - Current: Many bank accounts were added manually; transactions over a certain limit generate backend manual work for Austin.
   - Requirement: Automate the transaction process fully.
   - Constraint: Automation target is for transactions up to $50,000. Anything above that will remain manual for now.
2. **White-Labeling / "Web Skinning"**
   - Current: Platform is branded as Stairway.
   - Requirement: Build a "Portal" feature.
   - Use Case: A partner company called "Urban Genesis" needs a portal that functions like Stairway but looks like Urban Genesis to raise capital.
3. **Automated Education Flows**
   - Current: Austin gives manual, high-touch tours.
   - Requirement: A system that triggers short educational videos based on the user's "State of Mastery." As they progress, new content unlocks automatically to reduce 1-on-1 time.
4. **Formalized Auditing System**
   - Current: Users managing funds prove their responsibility via Google Sheets or Forum posts.
   - Requirement: Internalize this process. Build a formal tracking system within the app where Fund Managers must log/audit their deployment of capital.

**Known Bugs**

- **Onboarding Error:** During the demo, the "update_password" step was not marked as "current" for the Fundraiser user type.

**BA Action Items (Deliverables for Next Meeting)** **Do NOT ask the client about:** His current team size, his business model, what payment gateway he uses, or if he starts from scratch (he doesn't).

**Tasks:**

1. **Review the Recording:** Watch the demo meticulously to understand the current UI/UX flow and technical constraints.
2. **Create Understanding Document:** Develop a Mind Map and Solution Flow Diagram that visualizes the current ecosystem (Investor vs. Fundraiser flows) and where the new features (Automation/White-labeling) would plug in.
3. **Prepare Discovery Questions:** Since the roadmap cannot be built yet, prepare a list of deep-dive technical questions to fill the information gaps. Focus specifically on:
   - Edge cases for the $50k transaction limit.
   - Data structures needed for the new Audit Formalization module.
   - Customization limitations for the White-label portals.

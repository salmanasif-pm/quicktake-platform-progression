### **Problem Statement**

The objective is to integrate data from multiple healthcare systems to **predict staffing needs, generate team wellness scores, and optimize workforce efficiency**. Unlike traditional nurse-to-patient ratio models, this system will incorporate **task-based workload measurements, patient acuity, and staff experience levels** to enhance decision-making in hospital staffing.

This tool is critical for improving **operational efficiency, patient safety, and workforce well-being**, particularly in handling unpredictable healthcare demands (e.g., COVID-19, boarding patients, seasonal variations).

## Description

### **Problem Statement**

The client, a nonprofit arts and culture organization, is preparing for an upcoming music festival in June and needs a modern, user-friendly website that merges two outdated sites into one. The current platform is outdated, difficult to update, and lacks key features like online ticketing, donations, memberships, artifact sales, and content integration. They need a streamlined solution to manage events, engage supporters, and simplify administrative tasks — with a working ticketing system ready by end of May.

---

### **Technical Requirements**

#### 1. **Core Website Platform**

- WordPress-based CMS (current platform)
- Merge two existing sites: **Arts Link** and **Camerata Volcanica**
- Retain brand identity while unifying design
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop

#### 2. **Ticketing System**

- Launch deadline: **End of May**
- Online ticket purchasing workflow (festival-focused)
- Integration with **Stripe** (preferred) and/or **PayPal**
- Option for central payment processing (organization-level)
- Future option to route payments to individual artist accounts

#### 3. **Donation System**

- One-time and recurring donations
- Donation tiers with descriptions
- Simple checkout flow
- Auto-generated donation receipts

#### 4. **Membership System**

- Multiple membership levels with perks
- User sign-up and login
- Dashboard for members to manage profile, donations, ticket purchases

#### 5. **E-Commerce for Artifacts**

- Simple product catalog
- Payment and checkout integration
- Inventory tracking (optional)

#### 6. **Content Sections**

- Blog and podcast hosting
- Testimonials section
- Events calendar or listing
- Ability to update content easily via WordPress backend

#### 7. **Social Media Integration**

- Live Instagram feed or embed on homepage/artist pages
- Links to social accounts
- Ability to tag or showcase artists via social posts

#### 8. **Live Chat or Contact Tool**

- Embedded chat plugin or contact form for quick communication

#### 9. **User Experience (UX) Improvements**

- Simplified backend for content updates
- Faster load times
- Clean, modern UI
- Accessible and easy to navigate

#### 10. **User Portal**

- For donors, members, ticket buyers
- Login, history, digital ticket access

#### 11. **Security & Compliance**

- Secure payment processing (PCI-DSS compliance)
- SSL certificate
- CAPTCHA / spam protection

#### 12. **Analytics & Admin**

- Admin dashboard with reports on ticket sales, donations, memberships
- Google Analytics integration
- Export tools for CSV reports

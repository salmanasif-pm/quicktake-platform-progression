## Description

Murray runs a data platform for ~6,000 users across insurance companies and government departments in NZ. The government gave him SCORM-based privacy training modules and he needs somewhere to host them. Off-the-shelf LMS pricing was too expensive at his user count so he wants a custom build — a separate lightweight platform that runs the SCORM modules, with SSO from his existing .NET platform so users don't have to log in twice, completion status sent back to his system, multi-tenant org management so each client only sees their own users, and a PDF certificate on completion. His IT team hosts it on Azure.

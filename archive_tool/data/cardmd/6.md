## Description

The proposal should outline solutions for managing payroll, projects, contracts, procurement, assets, and financial processes. It must detail a system that supports multi-department payroll processing, tax automation, employee self-service (Incode integration), and compliance with federal and state regulations. For project management, the proposal should include tools for tracking budgets, timelines, and resources, with integration for financial data and reporting. Contract management should centralize records, automate approval workflows, and ensure compliance tracking. The procurement process should automate workflows from requisition to payment, and asset management should include tracking, real-time reporting, and compliance with accounting standards. Financial management should cover standard reporting, automated bill payments, banking software integration, and multi-year budgeting with budget-to-actual reporting. A detailed training plan and proposed schedule should also be included, with adjustments based on staff availability and feedback.

Meeting Notes

Need:

- Looking for a Software (iOS App for now as POC) for 3D heart murmur rendering. Vet Murmur app will act as a Library, including an AI powered comparison analysis module based on the custom trained set of Murmur from the existing database and AI Model gets stronger/accurate as users share data.

‌

Business / User Flow:

- **Device → Record audio file → BT connectivity → File upload on Mob / Web App → Existing Database of Heart Murmurs as samples → Comparison Analysis by AI**

**Step 0.**  Turn on the wireless murmur recorder attached to your stethoscope which is bluetooth paired to your phone/ipad.

**Step 1.**  Auscultate and get the best spot with minimal movement, amplify sound if needed.

**Step 2.**  Hit record and hold in place for 10 seconds (repeat if there was movement).

**Step 3.**  Text or email your murmur in the form of a screen capture video of the murmur file to Vet XPS, along with any comparison murmurs (include suspected murmur grade, age of animal, sex, species).

**Step 5.**  We send you back a video and images of the 3D rendered heart murmurs compared and graded for you to confirm your murmur in the medical record and to show the client.

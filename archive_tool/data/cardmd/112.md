## Description

### Technical Scoping Checklist: Healthcare Analytics Platform (Gerald Batist Lead)

**Goal:** Create a standardized checklist of **10 essential, high-impact technical questions** to better scope the proposed **Centralized Analytics Platform** for **Patient Outcome/Disease Pattern** prediction. This is to prepare for the next meeting with Dr. Gerald Batist and Farhoud. Please note the client did not allow meeting recording.

---

### Deliverable

A single, organized list containing **10 specific technical questions**. These questions must be designed to uncover key technical details and potential risks _before_ we draft the formal solution roadmap.

### Focus Areas for Questions (Mandatory to Include)

The ten questions must target the specific technical terms and challenges identified in the initial discovery notes:

1. **Data Ingestion & Standards (FHIR):** Clarify the client’s current adherence to the **FHIR standard** and the specific format of the clinical data received from the **EHR/EMRs**.
2. **Data Pipeline:** Define the method for transferring data (SFTP vs. API) and the specific requirements for the **FHIR to Google** transformation process.
3. **Security & Compliance (PHI/Consent):** Detailed questions on the procedures for **anonymizing PHI** data, and confirming the status of the **Patient Consent** mechanism (Is the access agreement finalized?).
4. **Target Functionality (Predictive Model):** Define the specific data points required to train the **predictive model to identify high-risk patients**.
5. **Existing Documentation/Access:** Confirm the availability and completeness of current access documentation for the aggregated clinical and operational data.

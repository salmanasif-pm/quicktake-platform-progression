## Description

The Prospects wants to have a **AI CHATBOT** to enhance candidate engagement and reduce manual workload from initial outreach, especially on their website. The founder is receiving too many manual requests, and some candidates get lost or drop off due to delays in response or missed steps.

She wants to:

1. Create a simple chatbot flow (basic conversation and options)
2. Use buttons or text to help users find the right info or book a meeting
3. Connect it to Zoho Calendar (just a link for now)
4. Save basic info like name/email if someone doesn’t schedule.

## Problem Statement

**The client, a home care services provider operating since 2008, is seeking to modernize and streamline its end-to-end operations using AI-driven automation.**

Currently, their processes — from client onboarding to care scheduling and caregiver hiring — involve significant manual effort, fragmented tools, and duplicated data entry. This not only leads to inefficiencies and delays but also increases the risk of errors and limits scalability.

A critical obstacle is the lack of a seamless, intelligent integration between external AI tools (e.g., voice assistants, schedulers, transcription engines) and their core system, **WellSky Personal Care**, which acts as the operational hub for scheduling, case management, and records.

# Technical Brief: AI-Driven Workflow Automation

## Overview

We are preparing a custom solution for a home care services provider aiming to leverage AI to streamline client onboarding, scheduling, hiring, and integration with their existing CRM: **WellSky Personal Care**. The goal is to **minimize human interaction**, automate repetitive tasks, and **seamlessly integrate** with WellSky through its API.

---

## Core Modules to Build

### 1. **Lead Intake & Contact Capture**

- **Input Channels**: Email, phone call (voice-to-text transcription), SMS, web forms, and social channels (e.g., FB, LinkedIn).
- **Required Data to Extract/Store**:
  - First name, last name
  - Phone number, email
  - Preferred contact method
  - Problem/situation summary (e.g., “Mother fell and now can’t be home alone”)
- **Processing**:
  - Auto-create a **contact record** in WellSky via API.

---

### 2. **Case Routing & Coordinator Assignment**

- **Logic for Assignment**:
  - Match problem category to an available **care coordinator** (from existing staff pool).
  - Optionally use skill-based routing and availability checks.
- **AI Functionality**:
  - Care coordinator is assigned automatically.
  - Notifications are sent to care coordinator with client details and issue summary.
- **Note**: The client does _not_ want the care coordinator reaching out manually.

---

### 3. **Registered Nurse Scheduling**

- **Coordinator Action**:
  - After reviewing case details, the coordinator chooses an appropriate **Registered Nurse** (RN) from a system-generated list.
  - Assignment is logged in system and **pushed to WellSky** under the client record.
- **Client Scheduling**:
  - RN’s calendar is sent to the client.
  - Client self-selects a time.
  - System sends calendar invites to both RN and client.
  - Add buffer rules (no double booking, minimum advance time, etc.).

---

### 4. **Intake Assessment Automation**

- **Meeting Type**: Virtual or phone (recorded).
- **AI/NLP Use**:
  - Transcribe meeting between RN and client.
  - Extract structured data from transcription.
  - Auto-populate **WellSky Intake Form** from this data.
  - Format should match WellSky’s schema (field validation, input types, etc.).
- **Compliance**:
  - Ensure all PII/PHI in this workflow is HIPAA-compliant.
  - Securely store raw audio, transcriptions, and forms.
  - Encrypt sensitive fields at rest and in transit.

---

### 5. **Caregiver Hiring Workflow**

> (Currently mid-stream on this; we’re extending/streamlining further.)

- **Automation Goals**:
  - Transcription of the conversation by the Care Co-ordinator with the Care Giver needs to be saved in the WellSky as well. This step can be further automated but at the later stage.

---

### 6. **WellSky Integration**

- **API Strategy**:
  - All contacts, cases, appointments, assessments, and intake forms must be pushed into WellSky in real-time.
  - Use WellSky’s **API Connect** to perform the following:
    - `POST /contacts`
    - `PUT /intake`
    - `GET /staff/availability`
    - `POST /appointment`
    - `PATCH /care_plan`

---

### 7. **Data Security & HIPAA Compliance**

- **Must-Haves**:
  - All PHI data must be encrypted.
  - Only authorized staff may access sensitive data.
  - Transcriptions and audio recordings stored with audit trails.

## Description

**Trello Card Title:** Louisiana Medical Group – Automated Medicine Refill Reminder System

**Description:**

- **Client Pain Point:**
  LMG currently tracks medication refills manually using spreadsheets and communicates with patients via Weave, which lacks automation. This process is time-consuming and prone to missed follow-ups.
- **Client Business Model:**
  Louisiana Medical Group is a healthcare practice providing patient treatment and medication management services, operating on a subscription and follow-up model that requires consistent patient communication.
- **Our Proposed Solution:**
  Develop an **automated refill reminder system** integrated with **Aesthetic Record (EMR)** to detect refill timelines and send automated SMS/email reminders. The system will include **two-way communication**, allowing patients to confirm refills or request dosage adjustments, with all responses visible in an **admin dashboard** for streamlined management.

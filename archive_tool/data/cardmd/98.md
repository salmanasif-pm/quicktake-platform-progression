**Requirements:**

   - Personalized gut health plans
   - AI-powered insights and suggestions
   - Tracking and logging (fiber intake, plant variety, health metrics)
   - Reminders for supplements
   - Optional gamification or engagement features for specific product lines
2. **Distributor-Facing (unique to Bellavie):**

   - **Ordering/stock management** dashboard

   - Suggest MVP vs full-scale development options.

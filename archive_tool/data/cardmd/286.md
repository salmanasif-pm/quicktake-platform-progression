## Description

The client is building a student-athlete wellness platform in partnership with a public school district. They’ve secured a pilot and now need a lean MVP rebuild with clean code, and HIPAA compliance (SOC 2 readiness support from ourside). The current platform requires full refactoring. Key user roles include Student-Athletes (daily wellness check-ins), Coaches (monitor athlete well-being), School Counselors (follow up on flagged cases), and District Admins (view reports, manage users). Tech stack used AWS + Supabase; open to recommendations. MVP delivery expected in 6 weeks with a team suggestion from our end.

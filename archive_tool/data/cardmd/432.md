## Description

A prospect is looking a Hybrid Mobile Application for a job-seeking platform designed for convenience, where job seekers can find skilled jobs such as cleaning, car washing, or house-moving services on an hourly or daily basis.

**Users**: job providers and job seekers & Admin.

‌

- **Hybrid Mobile Application**

‌

- The app will have two types of users - job providers and job seekers.
- Job providers can post temporary, short-term jobs or tasks they need done, such as setting up furniture, yard work, event staffing, etc.
- Job providers can provide details about the job, location, and compensation they are offering.
- Job seekers can browse available jobs in their local area, apply for ones they are qualified for, and accept the job if selected by the provider.
- Vetting and Rating System for Job Seekers:
- Job seekers will create a profile on the app with details about their skills and expertise levels (novice, intermediate, expert).
- Job providers can view a job seeker's ratings and reviews from previous jobs they have completed through the app.
- The app can automatically promote job seekers to higher expertise levels based on the number of successful job completions.
- This helps job providers make informed decisions about which job seekers are best suited for their particular tasks or jobs.

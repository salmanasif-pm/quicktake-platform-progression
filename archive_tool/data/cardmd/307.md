## Description

 I met a prospect who works at _Expert Patient_, Its a cancer education site that provides simple, survivor-created content to help newly diagnosed patients understand their diagnosis and treatment options.
She’s looking for an **AI-driven, HIPAA-compliant solution** to identify, attract, and verify a **high-quality audience** so she can prove traffic value to her pharmaceutical sponsors.
Her vision is to use **AI and analytics** to enhance audience precision, improve patient experience, and eventually create **AI-generated video content** tailored to each cancer type and stage.
Key challenge: achieving these goals while maintaining **ethical data practices and compliance**.

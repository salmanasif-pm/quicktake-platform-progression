## Description

‌

**Idea**

- Go through 100 videos
- Analyse the Recorded videos based on defined parameters and Provide the best 10 for the Job role.
- Get Training
- Reducing time to make decision

‌

**Users**

- Recruitment Staff Members
  - Access their data
- Clients
  - SAAS Product

‌

**Features**

- Login
- Dashboard
- Core Video processing
  - Video Reading and making sentiment analysis
  - Assessment
  - Scoring
  - Transcribing into PDF
- Score / Ranking -> Close to role
- Predefined areas
  - Based on role
- A system already built out for applicants, let say we have 100 applications and will send below form to collect information,
  - Send questionnaires
  - Send PDF Resume
  - For each Role,
    - Top 10 Close Matches candidates
- Now from Top 10 matches, it will send link (_may be already built out + fetch from database)_,
  - For pre-recorded video Capturing
  - With questionnaires shared by client for that role
  - May be given 24 hours for answering question (Time Limit for Interview)
- Video Catured - Run AI
  - Score it
  - Small Summary Assessment of recommended candidates
    - Predefined areas
- AI -> Assessment, Scoring, Perdiction, Video Analysis
- Logout

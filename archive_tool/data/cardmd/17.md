## Description

Develop a diagnostic visualization platform using 3D and 4D widgets to enhance the presentation of patient lab results. The MVP will focus on integrating with laboratory APIs, normalizing data, and creating an intuitive dashboard with interactive 3D visualizations. Subsequent phases will include 4D holographic projections, AI-powered insights, and cumulative reporting for trend analysis. The platform will prioritize HIPAA compliance, data security, and scalability, leveraging modern technologies like React.js, Node.js, and TensorFlow. Objectives include improving patient engagement, streamlining healthcare data interpretation, and deploying a subscription-based service. Next steps involve reviewing provided documentation, defining a technical roadmap, and initiating development in Q1 2025.

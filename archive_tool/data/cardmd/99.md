## Description


**1. Background & Context**

Lumondt is exploring startup ideas centered around global expatriate communities — beginning with **South Africans living abroad**.

After joining over **800 Facebook expat groups**, two consistent challenges emerged:

- Difficulty forming genuine **local social connections** among expats.
- Limited access to **familiar products or services** (e.g., South African stores or cafes).

The idea evolved into a **map-based social utility app** where users can:

- Share their **approximate location** (via nearby landmark, not GPS).
- Sign up using **Facebook or LinkedIn**.
- View nearby **people** and **stores** relevant to their community.

The initial MVP focuses on South Africans abroad, with room to scale to other expat groups later.

Please check the document called Project: Meeting People App for requirements.

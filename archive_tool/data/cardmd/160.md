## Description

**PROJECT:** 

Acquisition and Confirmation of Purchase Orders

### INTRODUCTION

The client company intends to automate the process of acquiring its customers’ purchase orders into its ERP system and subsequently transmitting the order confirmation to the customer, including product availability details, through the implementation of an AI agent.

Currently, purchase orders are received via email and one or more people manually enter the data into the internal ERP system. Afterwards, based on a set of rules/reasoning, a confirmation email is sent back to the customer.

The number of purchase orders to be managed is a few hundred per month.

- Email system: Outlook (Exchange).
- ERP system: Microsoft Dynamics Business Central.

The client must be able to provide the AI agent, over time, with business instructions such as specific availability indications, special rules on how to handle certain customers’ orders, etc.

These business instructions do not modify the process itself but serve as operational guidelines.

The system must not make mistakes regarding item codes, quantities, and/or prices.

---

### IMPLEMENTATION

The process is divided into three distinct phases:

1. Classification of emails that are/contain a purchase order.
2. Extraction of order data from the email or its attachments (PDF or Excel) and insertion into the ERP system.
3. Processing of product availability date and warehouse for pick-up.

The first part of the project includes **phases 1 and 2**. Phase 3 will be developed later.

The project must be implemented by building a workflow on **n8n** or [**make.com**](http://make.com/ "‌").
Email content analysis, purchase data extraction, and order confirmation drafting must be performed by the AI system (OpenAI or equivalent).

The AI Agent’s LLM must be designed with a **specific prompt** for the assigned task and must also consider a **RAG** (Retrieval Augmented Generation) mechanism using the TXT instruction files provided by the client. The AI agent must include tools to query ERP master data (customers, items).

The client will provide real examples of purchase order emails.

For the initial prototype, the client will provide customer, address, and product master data in **Google Sheets**. Extracted orders will be recorded in a dedicated Google Sheet and sent via email. Later, integration with Microsoft Dynamics Business Central will be enabled.

The client will also provide access credentials to their systems (first Outlook, later Dynamics).

---

### PHASE 1: Email Reading and Classification

An AI workflow must be developed that reads emails one by one (trigger-based) and identifies whether they are/contain a purchase order.

- The purchase order may be in a PDF or Excel attachment, or directly in the body of the email.
- Once identified, the email must be **tagged with the label "ORDER"** and moved to a subfolder named **"ORDERS"**.
- The AI agent must also take into account TXT instruction files provided by the client with specific rules (e.g., emails from [xxx@xxxx.it](mailto:xxx@xxxx.it "‌") must not be processed).

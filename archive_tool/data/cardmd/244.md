## Description

A prospect is looking  to create an inclusive job platform that connects deaf and hard-of-hearing job seekers with employers looking to enhance their diversity and inclusion efforts. The platform will function as a mini-CRM and ATS, streamlining hiring while providing an accessible, video-driven experience for candidates.

The Mobile App would be for job seeker and Employer Portal  would be Web-Based.

---

**Project Overview** Great Kids wants to digitize their existing parenting education curriculum (17 manuals across 5 sets) into a mobile app. They are transitioning from print-heavy delivery to digital and have no in-house development capability.

---

**MVP Scope**

_Parent Side_

- Login with child profile (supports multiple children/ages)
- Age & stage content (activities, milestones, parenting skills)
- Push notifications from professionals
- Chatbot to query curriculum content

_Professional/Caregiver Side_

- Browse curriculum by developmental domain and age group
- Search across all content
- Send one-way push notifications to parents
- Chatbot for quick content access during family visits

_Platform/Backend_

- TalentLMS integration
- License-based subscription model (1-year and 3-year contracts)
- B2B onboarding — agencies (e.g. Head Start) purchase licenses and enroll families
- Data access for Great Kids for research/outcomes (anonymized)

## Description

Had a call with the prospect and I’ve mentioned below their problem statement and the deliverables.

Problem Statement:
They has a complex warranty management system that manages  300,000+ jewelry products, each linked to a specific SKU and warranty number. These warranties must be updated after 6 months after inspections, repairs, and cleanings by affiliated retailers. The system currently handles automated email reminders for customers and retailers but faces limitations and requires modernization. Additionally, the company manages a retailer dashboard for 129 companies (with over 250 locations) but lacks compatibility with popular POS systems like "The Edge" and has under-optimized SEO efforts across their network.

### Key Technical Areas of Focus:

1. **System Analysis & Optimization:**
   - Perform a thorough review of the existing warranty management system, including the database, backend processes, and retailer dashboard functionality.
   - Identify inefficiencies in how warranties are updated and managed across the system, particularly around scalability and performance.
2. **Cloud Migration & Database Optimization:**
   - Evaluate the potential for migrating the system to a cloud-based infrastructure to improve reliability, performance, and scalability.
   - Optimize the database, ensuring efficient handling of 300,000+ warranty records and their corresponding SKUs.
3. **POS System Integration:**
   - Integrate the system with popular retail POS systems, including "The Edge" and other frequently used platforms within their network.
   - Ensure seamless synchronization between the warranty management system and retailer POS systems to reduce manual data entry and improve warranty tracking.
4. **Automated Email Notification System:**
   - Review and optimize the current email notification system, which sends reminders for warranty updates 30 days and 14 days before the due date.
   - Ensure that email reminders are fully automated, customizable, and trackable for both customers and retailers.
5. **Retailer Dashboard Enhancement:**
   - Improve the existing retailer dashboard, which allows retailers to register customers, maintain warranties, generate reports, and order more warranties.
   - Make the dashboard more intuitive and scalable, with an improved user interface and functionality for managing larger volumes of warranty data.
6. **SEO Optimization:**
   - Enhance SEO efforts for each retailer in their network to improve visibility and online presence, driving more traffic and better engagement.
   - Analyze current SEO practices and implement best practices across the network of retailers.
7. **Security Enhancements:**
   - Ensure robust data security, including multi-factor authentication (MFA) and role-based access control (RBAC) for sensitive warranty data.
   - Assess and improve current security protocols to meet data privacy and regulatory compliance requirements.
8. **Scalability and Future Growth:**
   - Design the system to scale with future growth, both in terms of additional retailers and an increased number of warranties.
   - Build with flexibility to accommodate potential feature additions and integrations as business needs evolve.

(Mobile Application is something that isn’t a part of this roadmap as of now)

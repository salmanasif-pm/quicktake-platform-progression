## Description

We are developing an AI-powered wellness platform that personalizes fitness programs through an AI-driven questionnaire, tailoring exercise plans to individual needs, mobility, and conditions. It will feature real-time posture and form correction using motion tracking via a smartphone camera, progress tracking with adaptive training adjustments, and an AI virtual coach for real-time guidance. Smart notifications will enhance user engagement by providing reminders and personalized exercise recommendations. The platform will seamlessly integrate instructional video content and offer flexible payment options, including subscriptions or one-time purchases, with AI-driven suggestions for upgrades.

## Description

The client needs us to build a backend and admin dashboard that connects with their existing BOOM,ai app (100k+ users). The dashboard should provide mood analytics (check-in counts, mood breakdowns, trends), retention alerts with red/yellow/green risk indicators, and exportable PDF reports, all while keeping student data anonymous (ID-based only). The backend must be secure, API-driven, and scalable. Future phases may include grant-focused reporting and AI-driven insights to predict risks and suggest interventions.

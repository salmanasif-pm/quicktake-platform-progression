Key Functionalities:

1. **User Management & Role-Based Access Control**

**Features:**

- **Multi-Tier Admin Levels:**
  - **Global Admin:** Full access to all data and functionality.
  - **Country Admin**: Full access for that specific country
  - **Plant Admin:** Access restricted to specific regions.
  - **HSE Officer:** Limited access based on assigned responsibilities.
- **User Profiles:**
  - Name, role, contact details, and assigned regions.
  - Activity logs for accountability.
- **Role-Based Permissions:** Define access levels for data, reports, and actions based on user roles.

**Purpose:**

- Ensure data security and streamline operations across multiple locations.

---

1. **Health and Safety Management Module**

**Features:**

- **Management Plan Templates:** Pre-designed and customizable templates for health and safety plans.
- **Policy Uploads:** Attach organizational health and safety policies for easy access.
- **Task Assignments:** Assign and monitor health and safety responsibilities for team members.
- **Compliance Tracking:**
  - Monitor adherence to safety standards.
  - Generate alerts for non-compliance issues.

**Purpose:**

- Centralize health and safety plans for easier management and compliance tracking.

---

1. **Risk Assessment Tools**

**Features:**

- **Dynamic Risk Matrices:**
  - Pre-defined templates for common risks.
  - Customizable matrices for specific projects or sites.
- **Incident Logging:**
  - Log incidents with details like location, time, description, and severity.
  - Attach photos, videos, and supporting documents.
  - Tag incidents to projects or regions for better tracking.
- **Risk Analytics:**
  - Generate risk scores based on historical data.
  - Predict potential risks using data trends.

**Purpose:**

- Enable proactive identification and mitigation of risks to prevent incidents.

---

1. **Subcontractor Management Module**

**Features:**

- **Subcontractor Profiles:**
  - Store details like name, contact information, certifications, and past projects.
- **Compliance Tracking:**
  - Upload and track subcontractor compliance documents.
  - Automated reminders for certification renewals.
- **Performance Metrics:**
  - Track and rate subcontractor performance on HSE criteria.

**Purpose:**

- Streamline subcontractor oversight and ensure compliance with HSE standards.

---

1. **Incident Reporting and Analytics**

**Features:**

- **Simplified Reporting Forms:**
  - Quick incident reporting using dropdowns and checkboxes.
  - Options to add free-text descriptions and attachments.
- **Incident Rate Dashboard:**
  - Track incidents by type, severity, and location.
  - Filter data by date range, region, or project.
- **Automated Reports:** Generate and share incident reports with key stakeholders.
- **Root Cause Analysis Tool:**
  - Document findings from investigations.
  - Suggest corrective actions.

**Purpose:**

- Improve incident response times and provide insights to reduce future occurrences.

---

1. **Data Visualization and Analytics Dashboard**

**Features:**

- **Customizable Dashboards:**
  - Display key metrics like accident rates, compliance status, and training progress.
- **Trend Analysis:** Identify patterns and emerging risks over time.
- **Region-Specific Insights:** Break down data by region or project for targeted decision-making.
- **Export Options:**
  - Export reports in multiple formats (PDF, Excel).
  - Automated email sharing with stakeholders.

**Purpose:**

- Empower data-driven decision-making and enhance transparency across the organization.

---

1. **Training and Certification Management**

**Features:**

- **Training Modules:**
  - Host interactive training content, including videos and quizzes.
  - Assign mandatory training based on roles or projects.
- **Certification Tracking:**
  - Maintain records of completed certifications.
  - Automated alerts for upcoming expirations.
- **Progress Tracking:** View individual and team progress on training modules.

**Purpose:**

- Foster a culture of continuous learning and compliance with HSE standards.

---

1. **Notifications and Alerts**

**Features:**

- **Real-Time Alerts:**
  - Notify users of incidents, non-compliance issues, or overdue tasks.
- **Scheduled Notifications:**
  - Reminders for risk assessments, audits, or training sessions.
- **Custom Notifications:**
  - Allow admins to send tailored alerts to teams or regions.

**Purpose:**

- Enhance communication and ensure timely action on critical tasks.

---

1. **Cloud Integration and Offline Access**

**Features:**

- **Cloud-Based Storage:**
  - Store and sync data in real-time for global accessibility.

  - Automatically sync data when reconnected.

**Purpose:**

- Ensure seamless operations across regions with varying internet connectivity.

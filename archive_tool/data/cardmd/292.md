## Description

I met a prospect who is looking to implement a QR code-based system that connects each of their industrial fans to a dedicated web experience. Their idea is to place a unique QR code on every fan, which when scanned, takes the user to a landing page with fan-specific details and a form to contact the sales or support team, particularly for inquiries around condition monitoring.

QUICK NOTE:

We can us a third-party QR code platform that not only generates unique QR codes for each fan but also provides a dashboard with scan analytics. Can you please explore tools like [http://QR.io](http://QR.io "smartCard-inline") , Beaconstac, or Scanova platforms that offer dynamic QR codes (editable destinations post-print), basic analytics (scan count, time, device, location), and campaign management. The idea is to avoid reinventing the wheel for QR generation and tracking in the MVP. we can later integrate this into a custom platform if needed. Please shortlist 1–2 tools with pros, cons, and pricing so we can align with the Prospect fast.

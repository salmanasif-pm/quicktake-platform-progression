## Description

**Objective**
Create a roadmap and V0 prototype for Real Garage Life / Automotive Lift Service’s scheduling optimization tool.

**Context**
Client wants an internal tool that helps schedule approved service jobs more efficiently across technicians, trucks, parts availability, and geography. Jobs are currently reviewed manually from NetSuite and ServicePro, then assigned to technicians through ServicePro.

**Client Problem**
They often have a pool of approved jobs across the East Coast. Some are ready, some are waiting on parts. The team manually checks which jobs are approved, where they are located, whether parts are available, which technicians/trucks are available, and how to group jobs geographically to reduce travel time.

**BA Tasks**
Please create:

1. **Roadmap**
   - Main Product and future enhancements (Optional)
   - Key assumptions
   - Open questions for the client
   - Required integrations/data points from NetSuite and ServicePro
2. **V0 Prototype**
   - Simple clickable or visual prototype for the core workflow
   - Dashboard showing approved/ready jobs
   - Job readiness indicators: approved, parts available, location, estimated duration
   - Technician/truck availability view
   - Suggested schedule output grouped by geography
   - Option for internal team to approve/edit schedule before pushing to ServicePro

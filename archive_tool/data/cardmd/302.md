## Description

Prospect is building an AI-powered cooking robot called Chefee. It’s not just an app it’s a smart kitchen system that can see, think, and act. The robot should be able to recognize ingredients through cameras, understand if they’re fresh or spoiled, and keep track of how they change over time. It’ll also connect with sensors and robotic arms to cook automatically.

Our part is to figure out how to make this work technically  train AI models to recognize food, make the system run smoothly on small devices like Jetson Nano or Raspberry Pi, and design how all the data (images, freshness info, etc.) will be stored securely either on cloud or locally. In short: we’re helping him build the brain of an intelligent cooking robot.

He has shared the Sample DATA with us. Junaid was also on the call so please connect with him and please CREATE A GROUP asap.

## Description

(IBMS) is seeking a modern, AI-enabled global healthcare platform to replace their current outdated technology. Their core need is to ensure patient safety and professional integrity through secure credential verification of healthcare providers, while also offering patients seamless access to telemedicine consultations, intelligent EMRs, secure payments, and AI-powered care reminders.

On the provider side, they require a robust credentialing system with automated verification, integrated with global compliance standards (HIPAA, GDPR, and regional laws). Additionally, IBMS envisions advanced modules like AI-driven billing systems, cross-border telemedicine portals, education/training hubs, and proactive patient journey tracking.

The platform must be globally scalable, secure, and adaptable to different healthcare regulations, enabling IBMS to expand its mission of transparency, patient safety, and access to quality healthcare worldwide.

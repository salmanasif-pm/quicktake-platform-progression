## Description

Prospect is manually labeling EEGs, with 400–500 still unlabeled, and her current AI only summarizes spoken notes. We aim to build a custom Vision-Language AI to classify EEGs into clinical categories and generate draft reports. Immediate next steps: She is going to share 10–20 labeled samples, and she and Kody define the initial label buckets. Junaid will then build a POC with a simple review UI, which we’ll iterate on based on feedback before finalizing the full roadmap and handover.

## Description

The prospect’s biggest pain point is generating quality leads that actually convert. We discussed a lead scraping & automation tool that could help by: scanning targeted sites/social platforms, pulling public customer data, categorizing leads (cold/warm/hot), integrating with CRM, and automating follow-ups.This could shift their focus from broad/expensive ads to intent-based, high-quality leads.

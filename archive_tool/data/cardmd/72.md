## Description

**🔵 Problem Statement:**
Struggles with early discovery of procurement opportunities in the federal ecosystem. [http://SAM.gov](http://SAM.gov "smartCard-inline")  and GSA listings are _too late_. RFQs often surface via internal, regional, or relationship-based channels.

Rusty _does not want_ typical AI vendor fluff (SEO, keyword scraping, etc.) — he needs deeper federal insight and access.

---

**🟡 Core Goals:**

- AI system that **detects / predicts** upcoming (even unpublished) procurement needs
- Works **passively** — like a radar — no daily logins
- **Avoid** consulting, subscriptions, or manual tools
- **Single setup** MVP → can extend later

---

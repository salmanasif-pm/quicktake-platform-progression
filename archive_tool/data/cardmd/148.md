## Description

## **Problem Statement:**

There is a significant gap in how mental health professionals update their availability for new patients, leading to frustration among individuals seeking care. Many clinicians lack an accessible, real-time system to indicate whether they are accepting new patients, and prospective patients often abandon the search process after encountering outdated or inaccurate information. Existing tools are fragmented, outdated, or difficult to maintain, resulting in poor visibility for clinicians and obstacles for those seeking mental health services.

## **Objective:**

To develop a web-based platform that serves as a real-time, user-friendly directory of mental health clinicians, enabling:

- Clinicians to update their availability status quickly and easily.
- Prospective patients to search for and connect with available clinicians.
- Automation and scalability to ensure minimal manual intervention for platform administrators.

---

## **Key Features & Requirements:**

### **Clinician Directory:**

- Public directory listing licensed mental health professionals.
- Clinicians can claim their profile by verifying their identity via:
  - **NPPES (National Plan and Provider Enumeration System)** or other state license databases.
  - Automated verification process integrated into the platform.
- Initial directory will be populated by the client (list of at least 500 clinicians at launch).
- New clinicians receive automated invitations to claim their profiles.

### **Clinician Subscription Plans:**

- **Four subscription tiers:**
  1. **Free Plan:** Basic listing with availability status (accepting new patients or not).
  2. **Month-to-Month Plan:** Auto-renewal with increased features.
  3. **6-Month Plan:** Additional functionality.
  4. **12-Month Plan:** Full feature access.
- Feature availability increases by tier:
  - Ability to update availabilities at-will.
  - Control over frequency of SMS pings requesting availability updates.
  - Manual profile modification through secure login.
- Subscription payments to be automated via **Stripe** or comparable payment gateway.

### **Availability Management & Automation:**

- Bi-directional, automated SMS system for clinicians:
  - Clinicians receive 1–3 SMS prompts per week asking if they are accepting new patients in the next 7 days.
  - Responses trigger updates to their availability status.
  - If accepting, clinicians are prompted to select available slots via a simple calendar interface.
- Daily system updates at **10 AM local time** for each clinician.
- Clinicians can manually update their availability as often as desired, depending on their subscription tier.

### **Site Visitor Features:**

- Access to directory is **free** for all site visitors.
- Advanced filtering options:
  - Gender
  - State(s) licensed to practice
  - Distance from visitor’s ZIP code
  - Service Type: Telehealth, In-Person, Both
  - Payment Type: Accepts Insurance, Self-Pay, Both
  - LGBTQAI+ Friendly indicator (vs. no data)
  - Years of Practice: <5, 6–10, 11–20, 21+, or Any
  - Populations Served: Children, Adolescents, Adults, Elders, or combinations thereof
  - Provider Type: Psychologist, Psychiatrist, Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC), Addictions Specialist, Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist (LMFT), or combinations thereof
- Ability to “click through” a clinician’s profile to:
  - Visit their personal website.
  - Access external profiles (e.g., Psychology Today).
  - Place a direct phone call to the clinician via the listing.
- Optional SMS alerts for site visitors:
  - Visitors can subscribe to receive SMS notifications when selected clinicians update availability.

---

## **Additional Technical Considerations:**

- Full automation of verification, availability management, notifications, and payments.
- Highly scalable and secure infrastructure.
- Minimal administrative involvement required from the platform owner (goal: under 1 hour/day).
- System must be U.S.-based for initial launch, with flexibility for future international expansion.

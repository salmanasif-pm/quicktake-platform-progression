## Description

Build a simple Astrology Web App with a Birth Chart Generator where users enter their birth details (date, time, location) to get planetary positions and house placements. The system should integrate **astrology data from external sources** (AstroSeek, APIs) with at least **200-300 years of historical data**. The **soft launch** will include a **minimalist website** (landing page, blog, and chart tool) with **basic horoscope interpretations** for user engagement.

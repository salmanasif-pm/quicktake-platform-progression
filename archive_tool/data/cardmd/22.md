## Description

[https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1RdkGaMZhnVNba-yQFiMUg1MTtxL4Ytv8](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1RdkGaMZhnVNba-yQFiMUg1MTtxL4Ytv8 "smartCard-inline")

The proposed CRM for the bike training workshop will provide a scalable and user-friendly solution tailored to manage customer interactions, bike service histories, and grant tracking. Key features include detailed customer profiles with photos and service records, automated communication tools for updates and notifications, integration with Xero for financial data, and an easy-to-use import/export system for existing data. It will also support tracking donations and grant milestones, generating reports for social impact metrics, and managing inventory of parts and second-hand bikes. Designed with affordability and GDPR compliance in mind, the CRM ensures streamlined operations and scalability for future growth.

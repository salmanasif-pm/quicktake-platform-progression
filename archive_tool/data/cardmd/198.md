## Description

## Problem Statement: Little Kitchen Academy (LKA)

Little Kitchen Academy is a growing education brand offering hands-on culinary experiences for children through physical locations. They currently use **Amelia** as their registration platform, which is deeply limiting their customer experience, operational efficiency, and long-term scalability.

They are actively seeking a **custom technology solution** to replace and eventually surpass their current toolset — ideally a phased solution starting with registration + reporting and expanding to a full tech stack including mobile apps and white-label potential.

---

## Key Problems

### 1. **Inefficient Registration Process**

- Users are redirected from LKA’s branded website to Amelia’s platform, breaking the brand experience.
- The registration flow is clunky, multi-click, and difficult to navigate.
- Filters and scheduling views are limited or non-intuitive.
- Customization of terms like “registration” vs. “enrollment” is restricted and expensive.

### 2. **Weak Reporting & Insights**

- Amelia uses Power BI for reporting, but dashboards are underdeveloped.
- Extracting data requires significant manual effort.
- No real-time view into metrics like class enrollment, instructor hours, or seasonal trends.

### 3. **Lack of Customization**

- Changes to class structures, terminology, or user roles require Amelia-side changes.
- Platform is not built for dynamic updates or internal control.
- Gift cards, discount codes, and upsell functionality are limited and hardcoded.

### 4. **No Integrated Mobile Experience**

- No current parent-facing or instructor-facing mobile apps.
- App functionality (scheduling, communication, alerts, etc.) is a major priority post-funding.

---

## Solution Requirements

### Core Web Platform

- **Custom registration system** with seamless, on-site experience.
- Advanced filters (e.g., age, day, time, location) with "Amazon-style" one-click UX.
- Fully branded with no redirects to third-party URLs.
- Support for gift cards, discount codes, add-ons, recurring and one-time payments.
- Admin control over terminology, age groups, pricing, and workflows.

### Dashboards & Reporting

- Real-time, visual dashboards for key KPIs:
  - Class registration by location/age/time
  - Instructor hours
  - Seasonality trends
- Exportable reports, preferably with drag-and-drop builder or low-code config.
- Ability to schedule automated reports.

### Mobile Applications (Not a must have but imp)

- **Parent-facing app:**
  - Schedule/view classes, receive notifications, manage profile, make payments.
- **Instructor-facing app:**
  - View shift schedule, track certifications, receive updates, message staff.

### Tech Stack Ownership & White-Label Potential

- Fully custom stack owned by LKA with IP rights.
- Designed for multi-tenancy (supporting white-label instances for other enrichment brands).
- Scalable infrastructure to support growing number of franchises/locations.

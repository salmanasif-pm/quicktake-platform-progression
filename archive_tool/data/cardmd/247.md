## Description

I met a prospect who has a E commerce platform 80% built by other tech partner. His immediate focus is on an **e-commerce multi-vendor marketplace** built on Laravel (web) and Flutter (mobile). Key requirements include UX/UI design enhancements, payment configuration, shipping gateway integration, and full-cycle QA. His main focus is on Design as he has a mood card and design which is willing to share with us.

I need you to come prepare a plan how we could help him with this processes. Taking up his existing web app and mobile app and revamping the design to make user experience better and doing some Integrations.

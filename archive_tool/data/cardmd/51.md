## Description

**Client Problem Statement:** Nick's sports medicine company needs a mobile app to help their patients (athletes) better follow their rehabilitation programs and stay connected with their providers. The current process of sharing programs via Google Drive and communicating separately is inefficient.

**Pain Points:**

- Patients struggle to follow their rehab programs when not in the clinic
- Patients have difficulty communicating directly with their assigned providers

**Work to be Done (MVP):**

- Build a hybrid mobile app (iOS focus) with the following functionality:
  - Patient intake form to capture injury details
  - Provider interface to create personalized rehab programs with exercises
  - Ability for patients to view and track their assigned programs
  - In-app messaging between patients and their providers
- Integrate the existing exercise program generator to populate the app
- Provide a simple, user-friendly experience for both patients and providers

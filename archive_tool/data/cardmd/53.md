## Description

**Client Problem Statement:** ADAA is launching a women’s mental health campaign in partnership with a national cosmetics brand and requires a standalone microsite to support storytelling, resource sharing, and brand collaboration.

**Client Pain Point:** The existing ADAA website lacks the design flexibility, UX capabilities, and brand integration necessary to effectively deliver a campaign of this nature, especially one requiring emotional impact and interactive elements.

**Work to be Done:** Design and develop a responsive, ADA-compliant microsite with engaging UX/UI, integrated resource sharing, email signups, storytelling features (like scroll-triggered moments or quizzes), and CMS training. Deliverables include moodboards, wireframes, design files (preferably Figma), and 60-day post-launch support.

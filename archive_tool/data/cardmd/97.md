## Description

Ryan has two main digital curriculum products:

- For the B2B Smart English product:
  - Implement AI features to make the product more engaging and personalized for students
  - Reduce administrative burden for teachers, such as automating grading and assessments
- For the B2C Route2Write product:
  - Integrate an AI-powered chatbot or tutor to provide personalized support and guidance to students

Specifically for the Route2Write product, Ryan is interested in the following AI capabilities:

- An AI learning coach or chatbot that students can interact with to get help, practice questions, and personalized feedback
- An AI-powered engine that can monitor student progress, quiz scores, and engagement, and then adapt the learning path and content accordingly
- Functionality to detect if students are losing interest and adjust the tone/approach to re-engage them
- A teacher dashboard to provide insights into each student's AI-driven learning profile and allow teachers to intervene or assign tasks directly.

The next steps are for PureLogics to investigate the feasibility of integrating the desired AI features into LearnWorlds, as well as provide a proposal for a custom LMS solution. He also asked for questions that he can answer in terms of requirements with his team for chatbot and LMS.

## Description

**Background**

Yang is the founder of [http://MediaFusion.ai](http://MediaFusion.ai "smartCard-inline")  — an AI-powered content company that produces multimedia ads and social media content for businesses. They charge clients per piece of content produced. If the content doesn't perform, clients churn. That's the core business risk.

---

**The Problem**

Their AI generates content blindly. It has no real-world feedback loop telling it what actually works. It can't distinguish between platforms (TikTok vs LinkedIn), niches (skincare vs SaaS), or audience types. The result is generic content that doesn't engage — wasted credits, unhappy clients, churn.

Three specific engineering problems they've identified:

- **Causality vs correlation** — the system can see a post performed well but can't know if it was the content structure, the author's fame, or just timing
- **True personalisation** — scraping a website gives niche-level guesses but not the real pain of a specific company
- **Algorithm uncertainty** — LinkedIn and TikTok change their ranking signals without notice and never explain why

---

**What needs to be built**

A system that continuously watches high-performing content across social platforms, analyses why it worked, and feeds those patterns back into the AI so future content is smarter and more engaging. Think of it as a self-improving content brain.

For now - we need a detailed proposal explaining how we would tackle this problem. If Yang sees the value - he would go ahead with an engineer. Please consult Ahmad Khalid as he was on the meeting

## Description

**Project Overview:** Development of a "Coach Jim" AI persona to automate executive coaching tasks. The client wants to move away from generic tools and build a branded experience that mimics his specific coaching style and methodology.

**Key Requirements:**

- **Virtual Coach Chatbot:** An AI (GPT-API based) trained on Jim’s 21 training modules, books, and PDFs to provide "What would Jim do?" style advice.
- **Branded Note-Taker:** A tool to record coaching sessions and automatically generate structured "60-day action plans" with specific agreed-upon steps.
- **AI Lesson Planner:** A tool for Jim to quickly combine different training modules into custom 4-hour facilitation guides.

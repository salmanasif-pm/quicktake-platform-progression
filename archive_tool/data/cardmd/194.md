## Description

### **Problem Statement: (**pediatric case management specifically for newborns**)**

Prospect is seeking an affordable, custom software solution for managing care for newborn patients, particularly premature babies. The software needs to track patient progress, facilitate communication between caregivers and healthcare entities, and manage the coordination of medical and social services for both the babies and their families. The goal is to develop a basic, cost-effective software solution that addresses these needs and is scalable for future enhancements.
Because they started very recently so we want to take the MVP approach so it is feasible for them to start with.

**Requirements:**

- **Initial Assessment:**
  - **Digital Intake Forms:** Simple interface for families to input medical needs, home environment, and support systems.
  - **Caregiver Profiles:** Section to manage caregiver details, including readiness and responsibilities.
  - **Assessment Checklist:** Predefined questions to evaluate social, accessibility, and cultural needs.
- **Customized Discharge Plan:**
  - **Basic Discharge Planning Tool:** Enable care providers to outline medical milestones, home care routines, and follow-up appointments.
  - **Resource Recommendations:** Link families to relevant community resources and medical equipment suppliers.
- **Family Education:**
  - **Educational Content Repository:** Store educational materials (videos, PDFs, guides) on newborn care (e.g., feeding, CPR, bathing).
  - **Checklists for Safe Practices:** Interactive checklists for families to ensure safe practices at home.
- **Care Coordination:**
  - **Appointment Scheduler:** A simple tool to manage follow-up visits and specialist consultations.
  - **Medical History Sharing:** Secure upload and sharing of critical medical records with primary care providers.
- **Ongoing Support:**
  - **Communication Portal:** A messaging system allowing parents to reach care coordinators for inquiries or concerns.
  - **Mental Health Resources:** Provide links to postpartum support resources and sibling activity ideas.
- **Progress Monitoring:**
  - **Growth and Development Logs:** Track and log newborn data such as weight, feeding, and sleep patterns.
  - **Notifications:** Alert for missed milestones or upcoming appointments.
- **Transition to Independent Care:**
  - **Roadmap Templates:** A feature for creating and sharing long-term care roadmaps.
  - **Milestone Achievements Dashboard:** A simple dashboard to highlight key medical milestones and achievements.

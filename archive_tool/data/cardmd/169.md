## Description

## **Problem Statement**

Endless Vitality ([https://www.endlessvitality.com/](https://www.endlessvitality.com/ "smartCard-inline") ), a licensed outpatient treatment center specializing in hormone replacement therapy, peptides, and weight loss programs, is looking to **increase patient acquisition** and streamline lead engagement.

Currently, all front-end consultations and lead follow-ups are handled manually by the co-founder, which limits scalability. Although they use GoHighLevel CRM, website chatbots, and SEO/Google reviews for organic growth, the **manual lead conversion process** results in delays and missed opportunities.

Jim (co-founder) is seeking a **cost-effective conversational AI solution** that automates initial outreach and qualification—without committing to expensive long-term SaaS contracts or monthly fees.

---

## **Project Requirements / Scope of Work**

### **Objective**

Develop a **custom conversational AI solution** tailored to Endless Vitality’s patient acquisition workflow.
The system should automate lead engagement, qualification, and scheduling, integrating directly with existing GoHighLevel CRM processes.

---

### **Functional Requirements**

1. **Inbound Lead Handling**
   - When a new lead submits their contact details via website forms or the chatbot, the AI should automatically initiate interaction.
   - The AI should ask predefined qualifying questions (based on Jim’s requirements) to gather essential information.
   - Responses and contact details should be stored and synced to GoHighLevel CRM.
2. **Outbound Call Automation**
   - The AI should be capable of making **outbound calls** to new leads in near real-time after they provide their details.
   - During these calls, the AI should ask a set of structured questions and record the responses.
   - All call summaries and data collected should automatically populate in GoHighLevel.
3. **Lead Management**
   - If a lead already exists in GoHighLevel, the AI should update that record instead of creating duplicates.
   - For new leads, create a new record and tag appropriately (e.g., “AI Qualified”).
   - Include logic to trigger reminders for Jim (e.g., “Follow up with Lead X”).
4. **Scheduling**
   - AI should be able to **schedule consultation calls** directly on Jim’s calendar based on availability.
   - AI sends a notification or reminder to Jim about the scheduled call.
5. **Integration with GoHighLevel**
   - Full two-way integration to sync contacts, notes, and reminders.
   - Push and pull lead data automatically without manual input.
6. **Medical History Form Linkage (Optional Phase)**
   - After qualification, AI should be able to send a link (via text or email) to the lead for filling out a medical history form.
   - Completed form details should be attached or linked within the CRM lead record.
7. **No Long-Term Contract / One-Time Build**
   - Solution should be a **custom-built proprietary platform** owned by Endless Vitality.
   - No recurring licensing fees after delivery (unlike SaaS AI tools).

---

## Description

Prospect needs an engine that can have their existing and new invoices gets transposed to a newer one that can be adjusted as per their mapping rules. It should be able to handle about 9000 invoices while each invoice can have an avg lines of 20000. Also, it should be able to integrate it with general ERPs out there. Be vary of HIPAA compliance in this as well.

To summarize the engine should:
\- Inout invoice
\- Transform as per the mapping rules/format
\- Export to certain ERP

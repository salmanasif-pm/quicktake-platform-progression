The app is an AI-powered planner and assistant designed specifically for caregivers and creators. Crystal, who is a mother of three, has developed this concept based on her own experiences managing the household, work, and childcare responsibilities.

The core problem the app aims to solve is the significant mental load and repetitive tasks that often fall on the primary caregiver in a household, typically the mother. Crystal has observed that in many families, especially in US culture, women end up bearing the brunt of organizing the home, coordinating schedules, managing chores and errands, on top of their paid work. This can lead to a lot of stress, burnout, and an imbalance in the distribution of labor.

The key features of the app include:

- Automation of repetitive weekly and monthly household tasks - things like meal planning, grocery lists, cleaning schedules, etc. The AI will handle the scheduling and reminders for these routine chores.
- Intelligent planning and coordination - the app will use AI to help caregivers better organize their time, delegate responsibilities, and find efficiencies in their daily and weekly routines.
- Integration of mindfulness and wellness - in addition to the organizational tools, Crystal wants to incorporate features that promote self-care, meditation, and other wellness practices to help caregivers find more balance.
- Community and support - Crystal already has an online community of caregivers that she plans to engage and get feedback from as she develops the app.

Crystal has already done a lot of the initial groundwork, mapping out the MVP functionality in Airtable. Now she is looking to partner with a development team, like PureLogics, to bring her vision to life. Her goal is to create a tool that can genuinely make a difference in the lives of busy caregivers by automating the mundane tasks and freeing up mental space for the more meaningful aspects of life.

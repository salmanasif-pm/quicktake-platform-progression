## Description

### Problem Statement

ShineOn Lighting is seeking a solution to integrate its inventory and pricing platform (Fishbull) with its Shopify-based B2B e-commerce site. The main challenge is enabling customer-specific pricing per product (SKU) within Shopify. Each customer has individualized pricing for thousands of products, managed in Fishbull. The public should not see any prices; only logged-in customers should see pricing relevant to them.

The client is also concerned about performance and scalability given their SKU volume. The existing system must remain fast and easy to manage as it grows.

### Goals

1. Ensure only logged-in users can view product pricing.
2. Display prices that are specific to the logged-in user’s account.
3. Keep pricing and inventory information synced between Fishbull and Shopify.
4. Maintain separate platforms for retail (public) and B2B (private) customers.
5. Ensure that the site remains performant with large SKU volumes.

---

### Functional Requirements

**User Login & Authentication**

- Users must log in before seeing any product prices.
- Once logged in, users should see only the pricing specific to their account.

**Customer-Specific Pricing**

- Pricing is stored in Fishbull.
- Some customers have pricing that overrides standard pricing per SKU.
- These overrides must be reflected in the storefront for each customer.
- Pricing should be pulled from Fishbull, either in real time or on a scheduled basis.

**SKU & Catalog Handling**

- Each customer may need access to 3,000–4,000 SKUs, with potential to scale up to 10,000.
- Product visibility may be shared across customers, but pricing is unique per customer.

**Platform Integration**

- Shopify is the current storefront and should remain in use unless a clearly better technical option is proposed.
- Fishbull is used for inventory, customer data, and pricing.
- System must integrate with Fishbull to fetch pricing data for each logged-in user.

**Order & Quote Handling**

- Logged-in users should be able to add items to quote or order based on their custom pricing.
- Orders should reflect pricing pulled from Fishbull.

**Security & Access Control**

- No customer should be able to view pricing assigned to other customers.
- The public should not see any pricing or be able to bypass the login requirement.

---

### Technical Constraints & Preferences

- Fishbull must remain the source of truth for pricing and product data.
- Shopify is the preferred platform.
- The team previously used Lilypad (connected to Fishbull) but moved away due to performance issues with large SKU sets.
- Alternative platforms (e.g. WooCommerce) may be considered only if there's a compelling technical reason.

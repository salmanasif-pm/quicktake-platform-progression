## Description

**TUB Platform — Developer Scope & Engagement**

The Uphill Battle Inc. (TUB) needs a dual-layer SaaS platform combining an invite-only peer community for independent healthcare executives with an open marketplace where providers and technology vendors can be discovered by buyers. The platform requires a single login environment, 9 subscription configurations (3 tiers × 3 provider classifications) with monthly and annual billing, a Guild Badge system, domain-specific peer endorsements, engagement-gated client ratings, and tier-based messaging access. All modules are required at launch including auth, profiles, search, billing, community forum, attestation workflow, analytics, and admin dashboard.

**Deliverables requested:** Full scope and build cost estimate, post-launch cost breakdown (hosting, infrastructure, licensing, maintenance, security, support SLA, and feature dev rate), recommended tech stack with long-term cost rationale, availability/support model post-launch, and notes on any costs that scale with user volume.

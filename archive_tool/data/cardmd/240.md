## Description

The prospect from the travel and tourism industry is looking for a Joomla-to-WordPress migration, along with a custom homepage design to enhance user experience. Additionally, they require the integration of a 3D interactive map, similar to Google Earth, with features like hover-based country highlights and better graphic alignment with branding colors. We need to plan the migration process, develop the custom homepage, and ensure the map integration works seamlessly with the site’s search functionality. We’ll discuss further details in the next meeting on Friday.

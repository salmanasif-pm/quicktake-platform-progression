## Description

### **Problem Statement**

Prospect is facing challenges in automating their operational processes, particularly around scheduling, lead nurturing, and client engagement. Current workflows rely heavily on manual coordination, which is time-consuming and prone to inefficiencies. The business needs a scalable, cost-effective, and integrated solution to streamline these operations, support growth, and enhance client experience.

---

**Requirements**

#### 1. **Automated Scheduling System**

- **Core Features:**
  - Real-time availability display based on team members’ schedules.
  - Integration of a customizable intake form for collecting client information during booking.
  - Automatic appointment confirmation and calendar integration (Zoom).
  - Fee slots based on session type/duration and display during booking.

#### 2. **Lead Nurturing Automation (Phase 2)**

- **Pre-Meeting Features:**
  - Automatic emails/SMS with helpful resources, such as blog links and FAQs, after booking.
- **Post-Meeting Features:**
  - Automated follow-ups with session summaries, additional resources, and reminders for the next session.

---

**Their Current Business Operations Cycle**

- **Lead Generation:**
  - Prospects discover the business through various channels:
    - Organic searches.
    - Google Ads or SEO-optimized content.
    - LinkedIn posts or other social media platforms.
    - Yelp listings.
- **Initial Contact:**
  - Prospects visit the company website and click on a "Contact Us" or "Consultation" button.
  - They fill out a basic form that captures essential details (e.g., name, email, phone number, and a brief message).
- **Scheduling Consultations:**
  - Upon receiving the form, the team manually contacts the prospect via email, call, or text to coordinate a consultation.
  - Several available time slots are shared with the prospect, and they confirm one.
- **Initial Consultation:**
  - A 15-20 minute consultation call is conducted by one of the team members.
  - During the call:
    - The team discusses the prospect’s needs and goals.
    - Key business details, such as fees and cancellation policies, are shared.
    - If both parties agree, an appointment is booked.
- **Client Onboarding:**
  - Following the consultation:
    - The team enters the client’s information (e.g., name, email, and phone) into their CRM system (plugin integrated into Squarespace website).
    - The client receives intake paperwork.
    - A Zoom link is generated and sent to the client for their first session.
- **Therapy Sessions:**
  - Regular therapy sessions are conducted with the client as scheduled.
  - Post-session, resources or follow-up materials may be shared to support the client's ongoing engagement and progress.

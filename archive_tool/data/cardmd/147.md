## Description

Prospect is looking for the custom nature “Athlete Management System“ as they think the ones that are out there in the market aren’t best suited for their workflow.

Below mentioned are the requirements gathered on the call:

**Dual-Function Structure:**

1. **Individual (1-on-1) Athlete Portal**
   - User login (athletes and coaches)
   - Calendar view:
     - Personalized mental training program
     - Uploads of documents, videos, assessments
   - Scheduling one-on-one sessions
   - Post-session summaries and resources
   - Communication/messaging portal
   - Stripe integration for subscription billing (monthly recurring)
2. **Team Portal**
   - Shared team calendar accessible by players
   - Team-specific training plans and schedules
   - Player-to-team assignment (drag/drop or similar)
   - Centralized content distribution per team
   - Player login pulls up the appropriate team environment
   - No billing integration required for team usage (handled externally)

## **Core System Modules & Requirements**

### 1. **User Management**

- **User Roles**:
  - Admin (internal staff)
  - Coach
  - Athlete (Individual or Team Member)

---

### 2. **Calendar & Scheduling Module**

- **Individual View**:
  - Personalized calendar for athletes
  - View scheduled sessions, training assignments, uploaded materials
- **Team View**:
  - Shared calendar visible to all team members
  - Coach can assign team-wide events or activities
- **Scheduling Capabilities**:
  - One-on-one sessions (between athlete and coach)
  - Schedule availability integration

---

### 3. **Content Delivery & Training Module**

- Upload and assign **videos**, **PDFs**, **documents**, and **training programs**
- Link content to:
  - Individual athlete's calendar
  - Team calendar
- Enable coaches to build tailored training sequences

---

### 4. **Assessments Module**

- Onboarding forms & mental/physical assessments
- Store historical assessment data
- Tie assessments to athlete profiles
- Support uploading external reports (PDF, etc.)

---

### 5. **Communication Module**

- **Messaging portal** for real-time or asynchronous chat (athlete ↔ coach)

---

### 6. **Payment Integration (One-on-One Only)**

- Use **Stripe API** for:
  - Monthly subscription billing
  - Recurring payments
  - Client self-service billing management
- Note: **Teams are NOT billed via platform** – external invoicing used by client

---

### 7. **Admin Panel**

- Role-based dashboard with access to:
  - User/athlete management
  - Team configurations
  - Content library
  - Calendar/scheduling overview
  - Reporting dashboard (basic metrics to start)

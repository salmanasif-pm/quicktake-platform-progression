## Description

The prospect wants a **custom AI-powered system** to drastically improve efficiency for takeoffs and vendor communications. The solution should:

1. Process large architectural and spec documents automatically, detect and bookmark glazing-related pages, extract relevant sections, and flag conflicts between drawings and specs.
2. Automate shop drawings and material lists, generate draft takeoffs so one drafter can handle what normally takes 2–3 people, while allowing edits to match company standards.
3. Streamline vendor communications, auto-generate personalized quote requests with relevant attachments, track sent emails, and reduce manual effort per job.

The core goa**l**: save time, reduce manpower needs, and provide clear ROI by making one person as productive as 2–3, instead of just adding staff.

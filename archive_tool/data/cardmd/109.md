## Description

**Healthcare Blockchain Platform - International Medical**

- **Purpose:** Build a secure, blockchain-based system for cancer care in developing countries, starting with Jamaica.
- **Core Features:**
  - Peer-to-peer patient-provider interactions without insurance intermediaries
  - Tokenized health credits for payments and insurance pools
  - Integration with EMRs and IoT devices for continuous patient monitoring
  - Automated claims, approvals, and payout processing via smart contracts
  - Strong data security, encryption, and anonymization of patient information
- **Design Goals:** Simple, efficient, cost-effective, scalable
- **Timeline:** Target commercial launch in 6–12 months

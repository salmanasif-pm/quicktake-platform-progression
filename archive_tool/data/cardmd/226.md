## Description

The prospect walked me through **Intelligence**, their live business intelligence platform that aggregates credible market, business, and economic data and offers AI-driven insights for entrepreneurs, incubators, and consultants. Version 2 aims to expand into a full digital business consultancy tool with more AI/LLM capabilities, automation, and enhanced reporting.

What we need to do?

1- Sign up for the 14-day trial and request full access to evaluate the platform.
2- **Analyze the platform** to identify areas for AI/LLM enhancements, dashboards, reporting, and workflow automation.
3- Analyze the gaps in the platform from the user’s perspective, identify opportunities to improve the user experience, and highlight areas where our AI expertise can add value.

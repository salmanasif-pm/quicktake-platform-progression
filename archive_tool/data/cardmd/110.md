## TerraVox Ventures Platform: Feature Estimation (Phase 1)

IMPORTANT : The Client is leaving for holidays on Friday. We need to complete this today.

This card covers the high-level estimation for the initial development phase of the **TerraVox Ventures** investment platform. This is to be prepared by the Business Analyst (BA) based on the comprehensive Technical Requirement Document (TRD v3.0) and the Client Meeting. Please watch the meeting in full.

### Client Pain Point

The client (Sarnian Group, Inc.) currently lacks a **centralized, branded, and automated digital platform** to manage their venture studio model. Their reliance on manual oversight for investor KYC/AML, ownership tracking, and payouts significantly limits the scalability, transparency, and efficiency required for their AgTech, FoodTech, and GreenTech investment verticals. The existing process cannot support the intended global growth (US, Chile, Middle East hubs).

### Client Requirement (Scope Focus)

The estimation must cover the core requirements necessary for a successful platform launch (based on TRD 3.0):

- **Platform Branding:** Implement the **"TerraVox Ventures, a Sarnian Group, Inc platform"** branding.
- **User Roles & Access:** Implementation of the Investor, Founder, and Super Admin role-based views.
- **Investment & Financial Engine:** Development of the Share-based ownership model, **Blockchain Transparency/Immutability Layer** (FR-2.2.2), and the **Fully Automated Payouts** logic (FR-2.2.3).
- **Compliance:** Mandatory **KYC/AML integration** and enforcement (T-6.1).
- **Access:** Development of the **Responsive Web Application** (Mobile App estimate to be separated).
- **Venture Sourcing:** Development of the **Public Application Portal** (FR-2.3.1).

### Metrics for a Successful Closing (Estimation)

The success of this estimation will be measured by its accuracy, depth of research, and alignment with the client's strategic needs:

1. **Fit to Client Needs:** The proposed solution and resulting estimate must strictly align with the requirements for **Shares-based ownership, automated payouts, and blockchain transparency** as specified by Sarnian Group, Inc.
2. **Depth of Research:** The BA must provide a justification for the chosen technology stack and estimation methodology, showing research into similar **InvestTech/FinTech solutions** and the integration costs associated with **KYC/AML providers** and the selected **DLT/Blockchain solution**.
3. **Estimated Timeline:** Total weeks/months for the full scope (excluding mobile app development, which should be optional/separate).
4. **Miro Map Deliverable:** A completed Miro map outlining the end-to-end platform flow, including the Investor Onboarding path, the Deal Lifecycle, and the Automated Payout process.
5. **Risk/Assumptions:** Clearly define all dependencies, especially those related to third-party integrations, data compliance, and collaboration with the client's internal stakeholders (if applicable).

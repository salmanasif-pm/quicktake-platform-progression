## Description

**Problem Statement:** Pickwell ([https://piqwell.com](https://piqwell.com/meet-your-therapist/ "‌")) currently operates a successful web-based therapist matching platform built on a proprietary seven-point algorithm, but it lacks a mobile presence and scalable infrastructure to support broader therapist participation. The existing integration with Simple Practice limits accessibility, automation, and expansion beyond Pickwell’s internal team. To address these challenges, Pickwell requires a HIPAA-compliant mobile application (iOS and Android) that replicates its current matching and scheduling flow while enabling a larger therapist marketplace, real-time calendar integration, and an improved, user-friendly experience for both clients and providers.

### **1. Business Context**

- **Pickwell** is a mental health group practice founded in **2021**, initially as a solo practice and later expanded.
- The platform differentiates itself through a **seven-point matching algorithm** that connects clients with therapists based on personality and therapeutic fit.
- The system currently operates as a **web application**.
- The goal is to **convert the existing web-based process into a mobile application (iOS + Android)**.
- Target timeline: **Q1 2026 (February–March)**.
- The future app will also act as a **marketplace** where external therapists and coaches can register, pay a fee, and receive matched clients.

---

### **2. Current System Overview**

- The existing Pickwell website includes:
  - A **seven-point matching algorithm** (custom-built, weighted question scoring).
  - A **consultation booking flow** that connects to **Simple Practice (EHR system)** for scheduling.
  - Clients can select a therapist, choose a session type (video or office visit), and pick an available slot.
- The backend connection between the Pickwell website and Simple Practice allows:
  - Therapist availability syncing.
  - Appointment creation within the EHR.
- Each therapist currently pays a monthly fee (~$100) to be part of the Simple Practice system.
- Paperwork (intake and consent forms) are sent through the EHR before the first session.

---

### **3. Forms & Documentation (Current Process)**

Clients must complete these forms before therapy begins:

- Consent for use of AI note-taking
- Credit card information
- Authorization for release of information
- Notice of privacy practices
- Coping skills and practice policies
- Client intake form
- **PHQ-9** (depression screening)
- **GAD-7** (anxiety screening)
- Demographics form
- Informed consent to treat

---

### **4. Functional Requirements (Discussed for Mobile App)**

#### **A. Client / Patient Functions**

1. **User Registration & Onboarding**
   - Simple and secure signup.
   - Intake form completion.
   - Optional insurance information upload.
   - Consent and agreement forms.
   - HIPAA compliance required.
2. **Smart Intake Form**
   - Questionnaire with structured questions.
   - The seven-point matching algorithm runs on client responses.
   - Weighted scoring system determines best therapist matches.
3. **Therapist Matching**
   - Matches displayed by percentage (e.g., 91%, 82%, 69%).
   - Option to view therapist details and select one.
   - AI-based or logic-based scoring, as per existing algorithm.
4. **Appointment Scheduling**
   - Real-time slot availability for each therapist.
   - Option to **book, reschedule, or cancel** appointments.
   - Session types:
     - 15-minute consultation
     - Psychiatric diagnostic evaluation
     - Coaching session
     - Video or in-person appointment
   - Scheduling should sync with therapist calendars automatically.
5. **Notifications**
   - SMS or push notifications for appointment reminders and updates.
6. **Dashboard**
   - View upcoming and past appointments.
   - Access completed forms and history.

---

#### **B. Therapist / Provider Functions**

1. **Registration & Onboarding**
   - Therapist signup with profile, qualifications, and license details.
   - Admin approval required before activation.
   - Option for non-Pickwell therapists (external marketplace).
2. **Calendar Management**
   - Therapist availability synced automatically (preferably with Google Calendar or Calendly).
   - Admin oversight of all schedules.
3. **Dashboard**
   - View upcoming appointments, client requests, and match results.
   - Access to patient booking and consultation details.
4. **Marketplace Participation**
   - External therapists and coaches can:
     - Register and pay a subscription or listing fee ($25–$29 mentioned).
     - Receive matches from clients.
   - Coaches are not state-restricted (unlike licensed therapists).

---

#### **C. Admin Functions**

1. **Control Panel**
   - Oversee all therapist and client registrations.
   - Approve new providers.
   - Manage listings and sessions.
   - Monitor HIPAA compliance and overall data integrity.
2. **Reporting**
   - Track total matches, appointments, and user activity metrics.

---

### **5. Technical Requirements (From Discussion)**

- **App Type:** Hybrid (single codebase for iOS and Android).
- **Languages/Frameworks Mentioned:** _React Native_ or _Flutter_.
- **Data Storage:**
  - Secure and compliant with **HIPAA** standards.
  - Store user profiles, therapist profiles, and match results.
  - Support integration with external EHRs or calendars.
- **Calendar Integration:**
  - Current: Simple Practice (for internal team).
  - Future: Generalized (e.g., Google Calendar, Calendly) for external providers.
- **Algorithm:**
  - Custom weighted-scale model created by Marcus and Vicky.
  - Each question contributes a specific weight to the final compatibility score.
- **Automation:**
  - Appointment sync and notifications should run automatically (to minimize manual admin effort).
- **Video Consultation:**
  - Should support online sessions (video conferencing capability).

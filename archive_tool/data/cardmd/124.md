## Description

Problem: 6,000 to 7,000 community pharmacies across 22 PMS systems have no way to push clinical activity back to physician EHRs. Pharmacists are prescribing and providing clinical services but communication to the physician side is happening via manual fax. No technology solution exists in the market for this today.

What we need to figure out before April 10th:

- Is bidirectional EHR communication feasible for pharmacist prescribing and scope of practice use cases specifically
- How does SMART on FHIR handle inbound writes to Epic for non-prescription clinical data
- What is the technical architecture for an overlay that sits on top of 22 different PMS systems without disrupting them
- What are the limitations of Surescripts for this use case beyond standard refill and prescription routing

Deliverable for next call: A clear technical position and preliminary solution concept to present to Gold and Jim.

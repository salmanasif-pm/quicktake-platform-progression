## Description

The goal is to replace their existing reliance on spreadsheets (via G Suite) with a mobile, secure, and HIPAA-compliant CRM platform. This CRM will manage employment specialists' workflows, case management, client interactions, and job placements.

The system will also streamline the process of migrating data from current G Suite tools and integrate seamlessly with the organization’s operational needs.

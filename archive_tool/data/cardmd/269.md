## Description

The prospect required an AI platform, in both website and application form, that would help students between age 13 to 19 to access their mindfullness programs that will be divided into short modules. The platform would also include an AI chatbot based on LLM model that would be trained to recognize student wellness needs. (focus on mindfulness and wellness programs and not exactly a specific subject) The Ai should also have language detection and flexibility as to how the animation looks like, giving control to students. Also prospect preferred for it to be some shapes instead of realistic animations. Students should have maximum control that means parents or teachers should not have access to everything, providing students privacy although the need for crisis detection in the form of AI detecting any violent nature or words used by the students should be red flagged and reported to parents/teachers.

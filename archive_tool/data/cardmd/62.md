## Description

Build a menstrual and reproductive health mobile app tailored for women with heavy flow, with AI-powered health insights, educational modules, symptom tracking, and community features.

---

**Key Features – MVP**

- **User Onboarding:** Google/Apple login, quick personalized intake quiz
- **Cycle Tracking:** Period start/end logging, ovulation predictions
- **Symptom Logging:** Mood, pain, fatigue, flow, discharge, sleep, meds, etc.
- **AI Insights:** Detect patterns (e.g., dizziness + heavy flow), suggest provider visits, generate health summaries
- **Educational Modules:** Life-stage-based content (first period → menopause)
- **Product Recommendations:** Suggest Avarie products based on cycle & symptoms
- **Community (Phaseable):** Topic-based forums, anonymous posts, mod tools
- **Admin Panel:** For content management, community moderation

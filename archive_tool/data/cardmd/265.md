## Description

I met a prospect who is looking to automate and enhance the Canada DREAMS Skill Assessment platform by integrating AI-driven skill evaluation with LearnDash LMS. He needs a system that can analyze user skills, identify gaps, recommend personalized training programs, and track progress—all with minimal manual intervention. Key requirements include AI-powered recommendations, automated course enrollment, certification issuance, and gamification features to improve user engagement. The goal is to scale the platform efficiently and provide a seamless, self-guided learning experience for skilled workers and immigrants.

We need to provide him with a High Level roadmap. Since we haven’t had a demo of his current platform which is pending from his side.

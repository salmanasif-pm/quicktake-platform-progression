## Description

The prospect want custom, user-friendly mobile app that gives patients a "one-stop" view of their appointments and meds. The problem is that oncology patients currently have no digital way to see their complex treatment schedules or medication lists, forcing them to call the office constantly for basic information. While they use a legacy system called ARIA, it is outdated and very difficult to pull data from, and their current third-party app simply doesn't show enough detail. They need us to build a custom, user-friendly mobile app that gives patients a "one-stop" view of their appointments and meds. Crucially, they need us to design this app so it can securely pull data from their current on-site database today, but stay functional even when they migrate to a brand-new EMR system later this year.

The challenge we accepted

We took on the hard part first

The engagement began with more than a software build. It required a reliable operating model across application, device, cloud, deployment and regulated-delivery concerns.

What had to be solved

A clinical kiosk with real operational constraints

  • Windows-first kiosk execution across physical devices
  • Detecto, Nonin, Dermalog and camera integration
  • Offline continuity, session integrity and secure cloud sync
  • Member and admin experiences around the same session record
  • Managed provisioning and installation, with update, rollback and release controls progressing
What the engagement produced

A stable, manageable and extensible platform core

  • Hardware-backed App 1 journeys working end to end
  • Operational portals, permissions and device administration
  • Managed provisioning through Microsoft Intune
  • A shared architecture ready to support controlled variants
  • A delivery model that keeps progress moving around dependencies
The value is not only that App 1 works. The deeper achievement is that the core can now absorb new products, integrations and operating capabilities without starting over.

Delivery philosophy

Disciplined expansion—not scope drift

The platform is opening new avenues because the foundation was designed and delivered correctly. Each next step reuses the core, protects the release candidate and stays explicit about its dependency and commercial position.

Path to controlled release

A sequenced path with explicit gates

The path remains clear without forcing dates onto dependencies we do not control.

Validate the complete bench buildLoad the stable App 1 candidate onto the prototype environment with available vital-sign hardware.
Align QuickTake and KISConfirm provisioning, responsibilities, interfaces, access and the facility test process.
Integrate remaining non-vitals hardwareComplete smart-board, recovery, power-control or related work when hardware and vendor inputs are available.
Close UAT and stabilizeDisposition client feedback, retest affected flows and document known limitations.
Complete the gated security-validation pathExecute penetration testing only when the stable-candidate, environment and UAT entry gates are met.
Authorize a controlled releaseUse approved evidence, runbooks, rollback controls and stakeholder sign-off to move forward.

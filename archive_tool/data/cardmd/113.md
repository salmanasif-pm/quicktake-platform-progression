# **[PRE-SALES] ERPVAR Technical SEO Audit & Quote Generation**

**Client:** Adrian Montgomery (ERPVAR) **Due Date:** Monday, Dec 23 \@ 9:00 AM EST **Platform:** HubSpot CMS **Tool:** SEMrush

---

### **📋 Context**

The client (Adrian) runs a 12-year-old site with ~20,000 pages. She is technically savvy and has a premium SEMrush account. She knows exactly what is wrong but finds the work "too tedious" to do herself.

**The Goal:** We need to provide a **time/effort estimation (in hours)** to clear her specific SEMrush Site Health errors and a strategy to increase her Domain Authority.

---

### **1. Technical Scope (The "Cleanup")**

_Estimate hours to resolve the following on 20,000+ HubSpot pages:_

- **Protocol Fixes (HTTP > HTTPS):** Bulk fix mixed content warnings. Thousands of legacy blog posts (2012–2015) have hardcoded `http://` links to images and external sites.
- **Metadata De-Duplication:** Fix "Duplicate H1" and "Duplicate Title Tags" errors. This likely requires manual or programmatic updates to pagination/category templates.
- **Legacy Asset Removal:** Identify and remove broken/legacy CTA embed codes (circa 2016) that are throwing errors.
- **Author Page Template:** Redesign the "Author Page" template to handle inactive guest bloggers ("Zombie pages") without flagging errors, or implement a consolidation strategy.
- **Crawl Budget Pruning:** Analyze the 20k pages and identify low-value URLS for 301 redirection or 410 deletion.

---

### **2. Strategic Scope (The "Growth")**

_Provide a high-level strategy/approach for:_

- **"Authentic" Backlinking:** She explicitly asked for a **Wikipedia-style** sourcing strategy. She wants to be cited as a "Source of Truth." _Note: Do NOT propose low-quality directory links._
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):** Strategy to rank in AI search results (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini). How do we get ERPVAR cited in AI answers?
- **Competitor Benchmarking:** Analyze the gap between ERPVAR (DA 19) vs. **Acumatica** (DA 45) and **"Top Sage Resellers"**. Where are they getting links that she isn't?
- **Keywords:** Optimization plan for: `Top ERP Partners` and `Top Sage Resellers`.

---

- [ ] **Hour Estimation:** Detailed breakdown of hours required to fix the Technical Scope items (Section 1).
- [ ] **5 Reference Clients:** List of 5 past clients where we successfully improved Domain Authority or fixed technical debt.
- [ ] **Backlink Proof:** Specific examples of high-quality backlinks we secured for those references.
- [ ] **Strategy Brief:** 1-page summary on how we will tackle the "Wikipedia/AEO" request.

## Description

### Problem Statement

Prospect is seeking a development partner to build a Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**,** a multi-platform (iOS, Android, Web) youth sports recruitment and networking app. The platform will connect young athletes (ages 8-17) with recruiters and youth sports organizations, streamlining the talent discovery and recruitment process.

**Key Challenges:**

- Developing a **secure and compliant** platform that meets child safety, parental control, and data privacy regulations.
- Implementing a **matching engine** that effectively connects athletes with youth sports organizations based on skill, location, and other criteria.
- Creating a **social platform** with sport-specific content feeds, profile management, and direct messaging capabilities.
- Integrating **third-party services** for ID verification (CLEAR or Persona), payment processing (Rise Payments), AI-powered content moderation, and advanced search functionality.

Expected Timeline: 3-4 Months

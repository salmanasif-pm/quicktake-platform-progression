## Description

### **1. Tokenization Platform**

He wants to add or rebuild a system that allows users to:

- Tokenize **any private/illiquid asset** (aircraft, cars, liabilities, etc.)
- Tokenize **fractionally** (e.g., turn a $100K asset into 100 tokens, or 1 token — user decides)
- Tokenize **liabilities** as well, with reversed settlement logic
- Support both **buy-side and sell-side** structures

**Tokenization must be user-driven**, not fixed by the platform.

**2. Full Marketplace Functionality**

He wants his MVP to evolve into a full production system allowing:

- Buying and selling of tokenized assets on a regulated marketplace
- Execution of trades with **compliance workflows**
- Settlement processes (delivery vs payment, etc.)
- Liquidity managed by more users joining (not managed by the platform)

  **3. Integration With Private Blockchain**

He needs the tokenized assets stored and traded on a **private blockchain**, not Ethereum.

He referenced:

- **R3 / Corda**
- **Hyperledger Fabric**
- Open to exploring options (not deeply studied yet)
  **4. AI Integration**

AI should:

- Continuously **scan the market for news and data**
- Provide **independent real-time asset valuations**
- Run **business intelligence workflows**
- Assist with pricing models and dynamic valuation

This is a core requirement

**5. Digitization & Document Management**

He wants:

- Uploaded documents to be stored in **SharePoint** today
- But eventually, documents need to be **immobilized on blockchain** for:
  - Persistence
  - Version control
  - Single source of truth

“Digitization + blockchain storage” is a long-term requirement.

**6. CRM Integration (Existing System)**

He already uses a CRM for:

- Client onboarding
- KYC workflows
- Documentation collection
- Sales + customer service workflows

He wants the **new trading/tokenization platform integrated with his CRM** backend workflows.

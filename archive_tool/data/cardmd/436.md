## Description

Client: Brian Kinney — KinneyMunro Wealth Advisors

KinneyMunro is a boutique RIA (Registered Investment Advisor) firm managing ~50 households (~100 clients) of mostly high net worth individuals. The firm is a small team and is actively looking to scale beyond 100 clients. They use Fidelity and Schwab as custodians, Practifi as their CRM, and Vanilla for estate planning.

**Pain Point**
Their client onboarding process is entirely manual and email-driven. It has two friction points — first, collecting financial documents from prospects before analysis, and second, the actual onboarding where clients receive multiple rounds of DocuSign from both the custodian and the firm with little context or status updates. As they scale beyond their current network, this process will hurt client experience and become unmanageable.

**Proposed Solution**
A lightweight custom onboarding platform that collects client financial information upfront, reuses that data to pre-populate onboarding forms, automates document requests, and keeps clients updated on their onboarding status throughout the process. Follow-up call scheduled for Apr 1 at 1PM ET to present an ROI-based estimate.

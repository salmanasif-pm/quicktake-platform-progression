## Description

Pamela wants an AI-powered coaching functionality that can have a personalized conversation with users to provide health recommendations and guidance, rather than just providing written content. She wants the AI coach to be able to follow up (once or twice a week) with users over time, continuing the dialogue and providing ongoing support, rather than just a one-time interaction. The AI coach should utilize Pamela's company's database of health information (biometrics, genomics, drug interactions, etc.) to provide tailored, science-backed recommendations to users.

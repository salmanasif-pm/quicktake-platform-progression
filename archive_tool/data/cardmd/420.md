Prospect is looking to create a HIPAA-compliant web platform that provides mental health resources and support.

- Symptom Assessment and Diagnosis:
- The ability for users to input their symptoms and receive a diagnosis based on the DSM (Diagnostic and Statistical Manual) and ICD (International Classification of Diseases) standards.
- Treatment Recommendations:
- The platform will provide users with recommendations for the best practices and most innovative research on mental health treatments.
- The platform will allow users to compare different treatment approaches, such as trauma-informed therapy vs. cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT).
- Physical Health Integration:
- Integrate the ability for users to order lab tests (e.g., for heart health, liver function) and have the results incorporated into their profile.
- The platform will Provide information and guidance on the importance of addressing physical health factors that may contribute to mental health issues.
- Regulatory and Compliance Information:
- The platform will empower and educate users on the regulations and requirements for telehealth/online therapy, such as consent forms and privacy practices.
- The platform will provide checklists and resources for users to be more informed when working with mental health providers.
- Live Support and Advocacy:
- The platform will offer users the option to connect with live advocates or consultants for additional support and guidance.
- The platform will potentially allow users to schedule appointments with these live support personnel.
- Provider-Focused Features:
- The platform will provide resources and information to help mental health providers ensure they are meeting regulatory and compliance requirements.
- Potentially offer services or integrations to support providers, such as IT/compliance support or insurance options.

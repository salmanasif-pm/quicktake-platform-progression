## Description

**Client Overview:** Stephanie is a BCBA therapist and owner of S-cubed, a Flutter-based platform for ABA (Applied Behavior Analysis) clinics. She is looking to scale the platform by integrating AI to handle heavy administrative burdens. She is highly concerned about system stability and wants a methodical, phased rollout.

**Core Requirements:**

- **AI Session Summarization:** Build an AI engine to ingest "pinpoint data" and therapist narratives to generate "compliance-ready" session notes. Must adhere to varying state and insurance regulations.
- **Smart Scheduling:** Automate matching between therapists and kiddos. Needs to account for time availability, staff training levels, and clinical experience.
- **Sandbox Development:** All work must be done in a microservices architecture/sandbox to ensure the live Flutter app remains 100% stable during development.

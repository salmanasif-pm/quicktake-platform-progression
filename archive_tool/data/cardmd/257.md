## Description

We had a meeting with a prospect who wants to build a smart AI system that works without relying on the cloud. Instead of using AWS, Azure, or GCP, he wants to process everything on-site using private 5G networks and AI-powered cameras. We need to do our research and provide him with the Viability and the cost.

### **What He Wants to Build:**

1. **AI-Powered Smart Cameras**
   - Cameras with built-in 5G SIMs (no need for Wi-Fi or wired connections).
   - Can be placed anywhere—factories, construction sites, airports, etc.
   - Detects objects, people, and actions in real time.
2. **Private 5G Network**
   - Instead of using public networks or Wi-Fi, these cameras will send data through a dedicated 5G network.
   - Ensures fast, secure, and reliable AI processing.
3. **On-Premise AI Processing**
   - Instead of sending data to the cloud, AI models will run on local servers.
   - This makes processing faster, cheaper, and more secure.

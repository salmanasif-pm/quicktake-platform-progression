## Description

They want to build a cross-border financial application that enables users to send money to Ghana and other countries securely and efficiently. The platform should function as a remittance solution with transparent fees, real-time transfers, and wallet-based receiving options. A key differentiator is the ability for senders to automatically allocate a portion of each transfer into a dedicated savings balance for the recipient. This savings feature could support fixed amounts, percentage-based rules, or goal-oriented savings to encourage financial stability. Overall, the vision is to combine money transfer and structured saving into one seamless digital experience.

## Description

## **Problem Statement**

Wellington Capital Management specializes in holistic business financial planning, offering services in tax planning, exit strategies, valuations, estate planning, and more. The current prospecting process is manual, time-consuming, and inefficient, particularly for identifying high-quality, niche leads that require education on Wellington's services.

James expressed that conventional platforms (e.g., LinkedIn, ZoomInfo) require extensive manual filtering and outreach, which limits productivity and scalability. The firm needs a system that automates lead discovery, improves lead qualification, and integrates AI to reduce manual effort while maintaining high lead quality.

The platform must:
✔ Automate data scraping from multiple sources.
✔ Apply advanced filters to identify high-value prospects.
✔ Utilize AI to enhance targeting and continuously improve performance.
✔ Be fully proprietary and owned by Wellington Capital Management as a long-term business asset.

---

## **Confirmed Functional Requirements (based strictly on meeting content)**

✔ **Data Scraping**

- Extraction of prospect information from platforms like LinkedIn, ZoomInfo, RocketReach, etc.
- Data points to include:
  • Company information and contacts details (for relevant persona)
  • Event attendance or relevant industry activity detectable from online presence

✔ **Lead Filtering & Qualification**

- Ability to apply multi-layer filters, including:
  • Geographic location (e.g., Texas or other regions)
  • Recent business activities (e.g., attended relevant events, purchased financial tools)
  • User-defined criteria to focus on Wellington's niche target market
- System must emphasize quality over lead volume

✔ **AI & Machine Learning Capabilities**

- AI to assist with:
  • Lead qualification beyond basic data points
  • Continuous learning and system improvement over time
  • Self-optimization to increase prospecting efficiency

✔ **Automation Features**

- Optional automated outreach functionality (e.g., email, messaging)
- Integration with scheduling tools for automated appointment booking

✔ **Ownership & Technical Architecture**

- Platform developed as proprietary software solely for Wellington Capital Management
- Not designed for white-labeling or resale outside of Wellington's ownership
- System to be adaptable by future company owners, supporting business continuity

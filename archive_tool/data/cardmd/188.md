**Problem Statement**

The current caregiver management systems in smaller group home assisted living facilities lack seamless automation and real-time monitoring, leading to inefficiencies in patient care, scheduling, and administrative oversight. Manual processes increase the risk of miscommunication, missed tasks, and limited visibility for facility administrators.

The **Automated Caregiver Platform (ACP)** aims to address these issues by integrating smart home devices, providing real-time monitoring, streamlining caregiver workflows, and ensuring secure remote access for administrators. The platform will be cost-effective, lightweight, and internally managed to reduce reliance on external IT support.

## **Technical Requirements**

### **1. System Architecture & Infrastructure**

- Cloud-based or hybrid deployment model for scalability.
- Secure database for storing patient data, schedules, and device logs.
- API-based integration with smart home devices.
- HIPAA-compliant security measures, including encryption and access controls.

### **2. Smart Device Integration**

- **Cameras:** Live-streaming with secure, role-based access.
- **Thermostat:** Real-time temperature monitoring and remote adjustments.
- **Smart Doors (Front & Garage):** Secure access with logging and remote control.
- **IoT Device Hub:** Synchronization of all connected devices with the platform.

### **3. Caregiver Scheduling & Task Management**

- Calendar-based scheduling for caregivers (daily/weekly).
- Task assignment and tracking with real-time status updates.
- Notification system for reminders and pending tasks.
- Mobile and desktop support for caregivers.

### **4. Patient Charting & Care Management**

- Individual patient profiles with structured input fields:
  - **Diet Plan:** Meals, dietary restrictions, and preferences.
  - **Activity Plan:** Recommended exercises and social activities.
  - **Medicine Schedule:** Prescription tracking with alerts.
  - **Bathing Schedule:** Hygiene and routine logs.
- Simple and quick data entry system for caregivers.

### **5. Remote Administration & Compliance**

- Secure administrator portal with multi-level permissions.
- Ability to modify patient charts, schedules, and smart device settings remotely.
- Logging and auditing of all administrative actions.
- HIPAA-compliant data handling, including encryption and access logs.

### **6. User Interface & Experience**

- Intuitive, lightweight UI/UX for minimal training requirements.
- Mobile-responsive design for tablet and smartphone access.
- Dark/light mode and accessibility features.

### **7. Security & Compliance**

- **Data Encryption:** End-to-end encryption for sensitive patient data.
- **Role-Based Access:** Permission-based user access and authentication.
- **Activity Logging:** Audit logs for all actions performed on the system.
- **HIPAA Compliance:** Ensuring all personal health information is securely handled.

### **8. Next Steps & Development Roadmap**

1. Define **system architecture** and tech stack.
2. Develop initial **prototype** for usability testing.
3. Implement **smart device integration** and scheduling features.
4. Conduct **security assessments** before deployment.
5. Plan for **future scalability** and additional feature enhancements.

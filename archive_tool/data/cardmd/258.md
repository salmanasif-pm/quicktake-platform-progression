## Description

The client needs GPS tracking functionality integrated into their existing WordPress-based website to enhance tour experiences. They require a user-friendly system for uploading and managing tour images, ensuring seamless updates. Additionally, they are concerned about streaming vs. downloading content for international users and want a solution that optimizes accessibility.

The immediate priority is improving the website’s functionality, usability, and design while maintaining its existing branding. But we need to first assess the feasibility of GPS integration.

## Description

- **AI-driven Sales Development Tool**:
  - Lead generation via AI (automated outreach via emails, phone calls, and social media).
  - Ability to send and track video content links for patient education.
  - Integrating AI for automated, compliant product queries and information sharing with patients.
- **Compliance with Regulations**:
  - Ensuring the AI tool adheres to FDA regulations regarding claims about biologics.
  - The tool must avoid making unapproved claims (e.g., about stem cells), referring to products only as regenerative or biologics.
- **EMR Integration**:
  - Integration with EMR systems like **Advanced MD** to track patient information and follow-up reminders for physicians.
  - Seamless synchronization of data between the AI tool and existing medical systems.
- **Referral System**:
  - Development of a referral management system where patients can refer others, and physicians can manage these referrals and track patient engagement.
  - System should offer incentives (discounts or bonuses) for successful patient referrals.
- **Proactive Outreach**:
  - AI to proactively reach out to patients via multiple channels (email, text, phone) for reminders, follow-ups, and product education.
  - Integration with social media for patient outreach, especially in new geographic areas.
- **Data Tracking & Financial Integration**:
  - Backend system to track product orders, patient information, and treatment details.
  - Integration with QuickBooks or similar software for accounting and tracking payment and billing processes.
- **White-Label SaaS Model**:
  - The AI platform should be customizable to be offered as a white-label SaaS product to physicians and medical practices.
  - Easy-to-use interface for physicians to manage patient information, orders, and billing within their practices.
- **LinkedIn Data Parsing**:
  - Ability to extract data from LinkedIn for outreach and follow-up emails.
  - Automated outreach via email based on extracted LinkedIn information to physicians in targeted regions.
- **MVP Development & Trade Show Readiness**:
  - The tool should be ready in MVP form by early December, as it will be showcased at trade shows.
  - The MVP should offer core functionalities with the ability to expand as per future needs

Requirement: Roadmap for the full version and MVP version

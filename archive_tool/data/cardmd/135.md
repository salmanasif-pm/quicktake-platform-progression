## Description

**Problem Statement:**
The prospect faces significant challenges in efficiently gathering and analyzing granular real estate data to support its strategic decision-making processes. While existing tools like COSTAR provide high-level insights, they fall short in delivering detailed, localized information for smaller, high-growth markets such as Kyle, San Marcos, and Waxahachie, Texas.

Currently, data collection relies heavily on manual efforts to compile information from disparate sources, including city zoning maps, building permit cases, U.S. Census Bureau data, and independent market studies. This process is time-consuming, inconsistent, and limits the ability to make timely, data-driven decisions.

The prospect seeks a scalable, automated solution that leverages AI to:

1. Streamline the collection and aggregation of real estate market fundamentals and demographic data.
2. Provide granular insights into project-specific metrics, such as population growth, income trends, job growth, and market absorption rates.
3. Integrate diverse data sources into a unified platform to enhance efficiency and accuracy.
4. Support the development of new tools, such as market trend reporting systems and equity syndication platforms, for improved operational performance.

The solution should address existing gaps in data coverage, ensure real-time availability of critical information, and be versatile enough to support various projects and decision-making needs across the prospect’s operations.

**Objective:**
Develop an AI-driven data scraping solution for automating market research and reporting.

### **Functional Requirements**

1. **Data Scraping Capabilities**
   - Automate the collection of data from diverse sources, including:
     - **Zoning and permit data** from city websites.
     - **Market trends and economic data** from platforms like CoStar and Oxford Economics.
     - **Demographic and population statistics** from U.S. Census Bureau.
     - **Independent market studies** and HUD-related resources.
   - Ensure support for smaller markets like Kyle, San Marcos, and Waxahachie, Texas, where granular data is currently lacking.
2. **Data Aggregation and Transformation**
   - Consolidate data from multiple sources into a unified format.
   - Provide capabilities for filtering, sorting, and visualizing data based on Keltera’s key market indicators:
     - Population growth.
     - Job growth.
     - Median income trends.
     - Real estate absorption rates.
3. **AI Features**
   - Employ AI to analyze and identify market opportunities.
   - Implement predictive modeling for market trends based on historical data.
   - Automate reporting, such as generating insights on market fundamentals and pipeline projections.
4. **User Interaction**
   - Dashboard for visualizing data insights.
   - Configurable alerts and notifications for key market changes.
   - Exportable reports in formats suitable for internal use and stakeholder communication.

**Current Sources that they’re utilizing:**

- **CoStar** - For aggregated market data and insights, although limited in smaller markets like Kyle, San Marcos, and Waxahachie, Texas.
- **RealPage** - Property management and financial reporting.
- **Yardi** - Potential consideration for enhanced property-level financials.
- **Oxford Economics** - Data integration via CoStar.
- **City Websites** - Zoning cases, building permits, plat approvals for specific locations.
- **U.S. Census Bureau** - Demographic and population insights.
- **Independent Market Studies** - For specialized data in certain areas.
- **HUD Market Studies** - For loan-related demographic and market information.

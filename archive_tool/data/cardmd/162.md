## Problem Statement

In Bangladesh, a large portion of the population — including small vendors, rickshaw pullers, and market sellers — still rely heavily on **cash transactions**. Many of these individuals do not have access to banking channels, POS machines, or digital payment infrastructure. Existing solutions (such as **bKash, Rocket, and Nagad**) are widely used but still leave gaps for easy, inclusive, and device-free transactions.

The prospect wants to address this gap by creating a **FinTech platform** that provides an **alternative to cash**, enabling simple, secure, and accessible digital transactions. The vision is to allow everyday users and small businesses to perform transactions without relying on POS machines or extensive banking setups, while also ensuring **security, simplicity, and inclusivity**.

### **Primary Features**

1. **NFC “Tap-to-Pay” Transactions**
   - Core functionality: conduct payments via NFC phone-to-phone tapping.
   - Simple user flow: enter amount → tap phones → payment completed.
   - App acts as the **payment vehicle**, eliminating the need for POS machines.
2. **Alternative Payment Options**
   - QR code payments (secondary option, not first priority).
   - Ability to make/receive payments without requiring additional devices.
3. **Ledger & Reporting**
   - Automatic tracking of daily transactions.
   - Categorization of income, expenses, and balances.
   - Access to daily reports without manual entry.
   - Functions as an “accounts department in the phone.”
4. **Voice Functionality**
   - App should support **voice commands** for uneducated users.
   - Voice prompts for confirmations (e.g., “Payment of 50 Taka completed”).
   - Must work in **Bangla and English**.

---

### **Payment Infrastructure**

- Initial launch to use **third-party payment gateways**.
- Preferred provider: **SSL Commerce**.
- No direct bank API integrations in Phase 1 (planned for later phases).
- APIs from SSL Commerce (and others later) to be integrated into the app.

---

### **Security & Compliance**

- Highest emphasis on **robust security**, especially given sensitive financial data.
- Requirements include:
  - Secure backend handling and storage of user data.
  - Multiple layers of security and firewalls.
  - Ensuring system reliability and trustworthiness for users.

## Description

His business is currently operating across three separate systems that do not integrate with each other:

- Salesforce – used for CRM, contracts, and DocuSign
- PeerVantage – used for ERP, accounting, and employee clock-in/out
- TeamUp – used for scheduling and calendar management

Because these systems are not connected, each new client or job must be manually entered multiple times, creating duplicate work, inconsistency, and delays. There’s also no single dashboard to view all customer activities, schedules, and financials in one place.

He needs a unified, automated system that connects Salesforce, PeerVantage, and TeamUp to eliminate manual data entry and ensure all customer and scheduling information stays synchronized.

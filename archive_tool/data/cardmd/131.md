## Description

The prospect is collaborating with the team to explore the development of a **custom AI model for bacterial identification** based on **image data**, potentially involving **single-cell Raman or microscopic bacterial images**.
The model should automatically learn and distinguish among multiple bacterial strains relevant to sepsis research.

The conversation focused on the **data balance, image quality, and clarity of classification goals**, rather than implementation or commercial aspects.
The immediate outcome is to define the **data and modeling requirements** necessary for preparing a technical solution to be presented in the next meeting.

---

## ⚙️ **Detailed Requirements (Extracted Directly from the Discussion)**

### **1. Dataset Requirements**

- The model will rely on **bacterial image data** as the primary input source.
- The team emphasized that the **most important part of a custom model is having high-quality data**.
- Images must represent **each bacterial strain in balanced proportions** — for example:
  “If 10 different bacteria strains are covered and 1,000 images are provided, ideally there should be about 100 per strain.”
- The dataset must therefore be **stratified** across all classes to ensure balanced representation.
- The images must be **clear, properly labeled, and representative** of real bacterial morphologies.
- Craig mentioned potential access to **academic papers and reference datasets**, including one focusing on **single-cell bacterial images obtained using Raman techniques**.
- Those resources may provide **example image datasets or methodological context**.
- It’s critical to **validate that the data supplied provides sufficient representation** for each targeted strain.

---

### **2. Model Requirements**

- The system will be a **custom AI model trained for bacterial strain recognition** from image data.
- The model should automatically **extract and learn useful visual features** through repeated exposure — i.e., self-learned feature extraction from images.
- Classification should be **multi-class**, capable of distinguishing multiple bacterial species/strains.
- The design must **prioritize accuracy and reliability**, as it may contribute to research or clinical decision support.
- The model must handle **balanced data** effectively to prevent bias toward any strain.
- The approach should be **scalable**, allowing new bacterial strains or additional data to be integrated into the model later.

---

### **3. Research Integration Requirements**

- The team should review the papers and datasets Craig provides — particularly those discussing **single-cell Raman images of bacteria** — to identify **applicable data structures, preprocessing techniques, and labeling standards**.
- Recognize that some materials may be highly technical; the focus is on **extracting directly useful information for modeling and dataset preparation**.
- The internal research team should be able to **summarize key insights** from these references to guide feature engineering or preprocessing choices.

## Description

These are the client’s current top priorities, and we need to focus our efforts around them for this phase. The key features include: **Multi-Car Booking** (supporting multiple vehicles with custom services per car to prevent off-platform revenue loss), a simplified **Website Booking Flow** (with guest checkout and backend integration to ensure pros get notified), and a **Pro Wallet with Stripe Instant Payouts** (so pros can track earnings and cash out immediately).

**Mobile App Features**

1. **Multi-Car Booking**

- Users can add multiple vehicles in one booking
- Customize service & add-ons per car

1. **Pro Wallet & Instant Payout**

- Wallet dashboard for pros (track jobs, earnings)
- Stripe Instant Payout integration

### **Web Features**

1. **Website Booking Flow**

- “Book a Clean” on the website
- Guest checkout (no login/account)
- Must integrate with backend so **pros receive job notifications via app**

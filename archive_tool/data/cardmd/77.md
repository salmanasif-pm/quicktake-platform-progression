## Description

**Otegrity AI HR Policy Tool – Key Needs**

- **Purpose:** AI-powered platform to help businesses check HR policies for compliance with federal, state, and local laws, based on location, size, and industry.
- **Function:** Upload HR handbooks (PDF), scan for outdated or non-compliant sections, and provide updated, non-legal advice recommendations.
- **Goals:**
  - Save time vs. manual compliance research.
  - Ensure accuracy using up-to-date public regulations.
  - Offer web and mobile access.
  - Monetize via SaaS ($20–$30/month).
- **Core Features:**
  - AI document parsing & policy mapping to latest regulations.
  - Continuous public-data scraping from government sources.
  - Gap identification & suggested updated language.
  - Geo-specific compliance checks per company/employee location.
  - Web app (MVP), mobile later.
- **MVP Scope:**
  - Web-only, PDF uploads, pilot in 2–3 states.
  - Basic AI regulation matching & recommendations with disclaimer.

## Description

Prospect wants to build a **healthcare-focused event platform** (like Ticketmaster for medical conferences) that connects doctors, sales reps, and organizers in one place. Attendees can easily browse and filter events by specialty/location, purchase tickets, and check in with QR codes, while organizers can publish events, manage ticketing, sell sponsorships/booths, and track attendees. The platform generates revenue through service fees on ticket and sponsorship sales. Admins oversee approvals, payments, and featured events. We’ll start with niche specialties (wound care, orthopedics) and expand nationwide/internationally.

**Project Details:**
\- Existing Drupal Version: 7 → Update to Drupal Version: 10

**Marketing Features**

**Intuitive Search Functionality** - The user must be able to search for the product or content in an easy way. Ie auto-suggestions to help users quickly find products, implement filters and sorting options to enhance their shopping experience

- Search features on Search page
- Products suggestions based on user preferences

**User Reviews** - Customers should be able to write product reviews. There needs to be a screening process to filter out/address negative reviews with the customer.

**Email Marketing Integration** – needs to be able to integrate with Zoho CRM. When users submit data, it needs to be collected and added to our CRM.
Automations on Emails based on Abandoned Carts,

**Social Media Integration** – there must be ways to encourage customers/web users to follow Norgen and share their favourite products and content

**Content Marketing** - it must be easy to create a landing page, blog or other content sections to share valuable information related to Norgen’s products and services. This aspect must be customizable, through drag and drop and with custom code.

**Lead Capture Pop-ups -** it must be easy to position a pop-up/pop-out on various landing pages for various marketing activities. This also includes being able to easily acquire email opt-ins

**A/B Testing –** It would be great to easily test page-layout variations, different CTAs and accurately capture the results in the platform.

**Abandoned Cart Recovery –** ability to implement automated emails to remind customers about items left in their shopping carts and offer incentives to complete the purchase.

**Product Recommendations –** We must be able to recommend products to customers based on their user-behavior and purchase history

**Content Personalization –** Further to product recommendations, personalized content based on customer behavior and preferences

**Discounts and Coupons –** We should be able to create all standard discount models. Also, the ability to add count-down timers and flash-sales would be beneficial.

**Referral/Loyalty Programs –** We must be able to manage different levels of Referral and Loyalty programs. From Basic referral to a robust loyalty system to an exclusive loyalty program for VIP/high value accounts
\- Referral URL + Referral Code
\- Loyalty Program (Basic program + Premium program)

**Product Bundles –** Allow customers to purchase multiple related products together. It should also be possible to discount full bundles and integrate with various promotions.

## **Customer Service Enhancement Features**

**Live Chat Support –** Must be able to integrate easily with existing CRM.
Zoho CRM provides Live Chat support

**FAQ and Help Center –** It must be easy to create a comprehensive FAQ section/ self-service help center to address common customer questions and issues. Furthermore, FAQs must be associated with the correct products and services.
\- Main page FAQs, Certain products page FAQs

**Order Tracking –** Provide customers with order tracking capabilities, allowing them to monitor the status of their shipments. They could view this information buy visiting their online web profile.
\- FedEx software integration for shipping prices

**Multilingual Support**
\- (french, sp, germam, arabic, hindi, jp, mandarin)

**Customer Feedback Surveys –** From the point of purchase, an automatic survey should be sent for feedback after a set amount of days. The information gathered should organized in a way in which the appropriate action can be taken based on the nature of the feedback. There should also be options to add a pop-up on specific pages to collect information in a lykert scale.

**Additional Features:**

- Cart PopUp with Ajax
- Favorite a product
- Heatmap and User Activity analysis tool

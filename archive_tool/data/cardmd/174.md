## Description

## **Problem Statement**

Gatehouse, a property development firm with projects across Canada and the U.S., aims to gain a competitive edge in identifying **off-market real estate opportunities** before they reach public listings or traditional brokers.

Their vision is to create an **AI-based property discovery platform** capable of scanning and aggregating property data from multiple public and semi-public sources (MLS, Zillow, property databases, etc.) to locate **specific property types** that meet certain business criteria.

The system should help Gatehouse:

- Discover **undervalued or unlisted properties** that last appeared on MLS 15–20 years ago and haven’t been re-listed.
- Focus on **property owners nearing retirement age**, who may be open to selling or partnership opportunities.
- **Bypass brokers and middlemen** to negotiate directly with owners.
- Generate a **structured, searchable list of leads** with property and ownership details.

The end goal is to provide Gatehouse with **early access to high-value properties** (e.g., trailer parks, car washes, land for development) across business-friendly U.S. states — specifically **Florida, Texas, and Tennessee** — allowing proactive outreach and deal creation.

## 🧠 **Functional Requirements**

### 1. **Data Sources & Integration**

- Integrate with multiple **real estate data platforms**:
  - **MLS (Multiple Listing Service)**
  - **Zillow**
  - **Other public or regional property listing sources**
- Extract property data including:
  - Property address, type, previous listing date(s)
  - Ownership records (if available)
  - Current status (listed/unlisted)
  - Contact or tax mailing information (where permissible)
- Include **filters and parameters** for:
  - Properties listed **15–20 years ago** but **not re-listed since**
  - Specific **property categories** (e.g., trailer parks, car washes, undeveloped land)
  - **Geographic scope:** initially limited to **Florida, Texas, and Tennessee**

### 2. **Search & Filtering Engine**

- Build an internal search function allowing:
  - Dynamic filters (date range, state, property type)
  - Natural-language-style queries (optional future enhancement)
- Optionally employ **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** for natural query interpretation:
  - Example: “Show me trailer parks in Texas not listed since 2008.”
- Generate result lists with detailed property records.

### 3. **Data Output & Updates**

- Output property lists in structured format:
  - CSV / Excel export
  - API or dashboard view
- Include metadata:
  - Property name/address
  - Year last listed
  - Owner details (if public)
  - Source (MLS, Zillow, etc.)
- **Automated refresh cycle** (monthly or quarterly) to re-run filters and update the dataset.

### 4. **AI/Automation Layer**

- While AI is not central to the initial MVP, apply **AI for automation and enrichment**, such as:
  - Parsing and cleaning heterogeneous data sources
  - Deduplication and normalization of property data
  - Potential future enhancement: predictive scoring of “likely-to-sell” properties based on age, region, and ownership history.

## Description

**Problem Statement**

To enhance the current **Platform** by introducing **gamification and personalization** features that make the learning experience more **engaging, interactive, and immersive** for female professionals.

---

### **Current Platform Overview**

- MVP already exists with a **basic personalized learning roadmap**.
- Built as an **online learning platform** with access to training materials.
- Currently lacks **interactivity and engagement** features.
- Users do not have **clear progress tracking mechanisms**, leading to lower retention.
- Platform technology details are documented and can be shared.

---

### **Key Features to Develop**

#### **1. Gamification & Personalization**

- **Avatar Creation**: Users create and customize their avatars.
- **Level Progression System**: Users level up based on completed tasks.
- **Badges & Achievements**: Rewards for completing courses or milestones.
- **Interactive Roadmap**: A visual representation of user progress.
- **AI-Powered Recommendations**: Suggest relevant learning paths based on progress.
- **Leaderboards & Challenges**: Encouraging engagement and competition.

#### **2. User Engagement & Retention**

- **Notifications & Reminders**: Automated messages to encourage course completion.
- **Community Features**: Discussion forums, mentorship, and group challenges.
- **Incentives for Engagement**: Unlockable content, rewards, and social recognition.

#### **3. Platform Enhancements**

- **Tech Stack Assessment**: Ensure the platform supports new features efficiently.
- **Integration with Existing Systems**: Ensure compatibility with the current infrastructure.
- **Scalability Considerations**: Plan for long-term growth and increased user base.

#### **4. AI & Data Utilization**

- **AI for Personalized Learning**: Adaptive course recommendations.
- **Analytics Dashboard**: User engagement and progress tracking.
- **Behavior-Based Triggers**: AI-driven engagement prompts.

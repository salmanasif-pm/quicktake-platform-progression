## Description

### **Problem Statement**

RGB currently operates its production pipeline using a series of **Excel spreadsheets and macros**, developed over 20+ years by a single internal expert. These macros automate key workflows across departments including sales, engineering, detailing, and manufacturing. However, the system is becoming increasingly unstable due to **Windows security updates** and lack of support, causing critical programs to fail unexpectedly.

The current setup:

- Relies heavily on **manual processes**
- Is **highly fragile** due to single-user dependency and lack of documentation
- Involves **redundant data entry** across 8+ internal tools
- Cannot be reliably maintained post-retirement of the current owner

RGB is seeking to replace this fragile and outdated system with a **custom-built, web-based platform** that consolidates and streamlines their core production workflows in a robust, user-friendly, and future-proof manner.
They currently store everything on-premis servers.

### **Technical Requirements for the New Web-Based Platform**

#### 1. **Architecture & Platform**

- **Web-based application** (browser-accessible)
- Must be **independent of Windows OS** (no reliance on Excel/macros/VBA)
- Built using modern, maintainable frameworks.
- Self-hosted or cloud-hosted with full ownership by RGB

#### 2. **Core Functional Modules**

Each of the following modules reflects one of the legacy tools/processes currently used:

- **Job Intake & BOM Management**
  - Ingests **design output** (Bill of Materials) from **MDS software**
  - Supports parsing and standardizing BOM for use in downstream modules
  - Handles both standard and custom parts
- **Estimate vs. Design (EVD) Module**
  - Compares sales estimates with finalized engineering designs
  - Displays cost variance and flags discrepancies
  - Allows manual override or adjustment
- **Detailing Interface**
  - Enables detailing department to define part-specific data
  - Supports 80+ data input fields/tabs (equivalent to current e-shipper setup)
  - Handles logic for part fabrication instructions (holes, bends, material type, colors, etc.)
- **Shipper/Logistics Builder**
  - Aggregates finalized part data into “shippers” for transport
  - Organizes components by weld sets, accessories, fasteners, etc.
  - Outputs clean **CSV files** to be sent to the shop floor systems
- **Purchasing Module**
  - Generates pre-release material lists from BOM
  - Supports lead time and raw material requirements
  - Not connected to ERP; generates internal data for procurement team

#### 3. **Data Model & Storage**

- Centralized, normalized **relational database**
- Must support:
  - Job-level tracking (unique job ID)
  - Part-level specs
  - Quantity breakdowns
  - Variant configurations (color, coatings, etc.)

#### 4. **User Roles & Interfaces**

- Role-based access (Engineering, Detailing, Sales, Admin)
- Interfaces tailored per department
- Lightweight, fast-loading, and designed for internal teams with minimal training

#### 5. **Data Import/Export**

- Import from MDS (likely structured file formats – confirm JSON, XML, or CSV)
- Export as CSV for downstream use (e.g., shop floor machinery, external tools)
- No ERP integration required

#### 6. **Scalability & Maintainability**

- Modular design to accommodate future enhancements
- Well-documented codebase and business logic
- Flexible enough to accommodate ~200 unique parts per job and large volume variations (small to very large buildings)

#### 7. **Support for Complex Fabrication Logic**

- Rules-based part processing (e.g., galvanization, unique welds, accessories)
- Allow for part-specific exceptions and overrides
- Needs to replicate nuanced rules currently buried in Excel macros

#### 8. **Collaboration with Existing Expert**

- Platform development must include direct collaboration with the current system owner (the retiring prospect)
- He will assist with **spec writing**, rule definitions, testing, and validation

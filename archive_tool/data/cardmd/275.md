## Description

The prospect is looking to build a ride-sharing application with a strong focus on payment automation, driver wallet management, safety features, and admin reporting. Key requirements include support for cash and card payments, a driver wallet system with automatic 10% commission deduction, and wallet restrictions like overdraft protection and suspension on zero balance. The system must generate per-trip invoices, provide weekly/monthly earnings reports, and support local Jamaican payment gateways(Need to do some research). On the safety front, it should offer automatic audio recording on the driver’s side and optional video recording controlled by the rider, with cloud storage retention of 5–7 days. The solution should work across Android, iOS, and web, include a full admin panel, and prioritize secure integration of payments and data storage.

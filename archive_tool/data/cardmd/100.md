## Description

## **1. Background & Context**

CliniLink Ltd is a UK-based healthcare education platform founded by Mehmoona, a practicing pharmacist.
The company currently provides free CPD (Continuing Professional Development) events and clinical training for healthcare professionals.

As part of an accelerator program, CliniLink is developing an **AI-driven tool** to help clinicians and trainees access evidence-based answers to clinical questions derived from **NICE (National Institute for Health and Care Excellence)** guidelines.

This project is currently **self-funded** and in an early validation phase, requiring a **cost-effective and efficient MVP** that demonstrates both technical feasibility and clinical relevance.

---

## **2. Project Objectives**

The primary goal of this MVP is to build a **web-based AI assistant** capable of answering clinical questions using verified information from NICE guidelines.
The system should return concise, evidence-backed responses with citations and direct reference links.

The MVP will act as a **proof of concept** for potential investors and healthcare partners within the accelerator program.

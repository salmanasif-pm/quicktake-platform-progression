**DISCOVERY CALL NOTES**

Discovery call has been done with Ahmad and Jahangir and we’ve gathered all the requirements in terms of what he needs for an MVP and down the lane features.

The prospect is looking to develop a web and mobile application for a parking management system with functionality to book the available parking slots for any business building with below features:
\- Admin Control of the platform. (Web Interface)
\- Building Admin control to manage the employee parking. (Web Interface)
\- Hybrid Mobile Application for Employee usage.
\- Calendar for the parking slots reservation.
\- Building Admin will define the fixed and shared parking slots that employee can choose from.
\- It’ll have unit spaces not the parking map to choose from.
\- Terms and conditions to cater to the privacy policy for user data.
\- Reporting and Analytics functionality with a dashboad.

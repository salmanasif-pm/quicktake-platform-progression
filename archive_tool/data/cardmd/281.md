## Description

One of the prospect I met is building an AI sales agent, a digital person that can talk to customers, explain products, and even close deals. But he’s running into a few problems.

**The avatar doesn’t look real when talking.**
 The mouth movements (lip sync) don’t match the words very well, so the face looks robotic or off. He’s trying to fix that with NVIDIA's face-to-voice technology, but it still needs improvement.


The **top priority right now** is to get the lip sync feature working well. That is, making sure the video avatar's lips move in sync with the AI's speech and look natural and realistic.

Please review NVIDIA’s text-to-face API and any open-source alternatives we can leverage. Let’s also assess what it’ll take (in terms of engineering effort) to improve real-time lip sync using their existing AI pipeline

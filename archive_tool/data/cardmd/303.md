## Description

Prospecr wants a complete rebuild of Docs of Cannabis, not just cosmetic fixes. His top priority is a mandatory state-gating system above the fold, forcing users to pick their state before continuing. Each state must have tailored pages with clear pricing, laws, benefits, and strong CTAs like “Get Your Card” or “Renew License.” The current schema incorrectly lists them as a medical organization/telehealth provider; it needs correction to reflect their true role as a business operator/connector between patients and doctors. He also needs the site fully aligned with their third-party scheduling and escrow app to ensure a seamless user journey.

A major focus is SEO and technical performance, faster loading, improved Core Web Vitals, structured data, and authoritative metadata to recover lost traffic and rank better with search bots. Content is written by their SMEs, but blogs and informational pages need modern layouts and presentation. Design inspiration is [http://Leafwell.com](http://Leafwell.com "smartCard-inline") : clean, modern, state-driven flows with bold CTAs. Branding is flexible—his wife (creative director) will have final input, and the logo color doesn’t have to dictate the palette. Finally, ease of editing is crucial: team must prepare a side-by-side comparison of WordPress + Elementor vs HubSpot CMS, since Eric wants to make fast changes himself without relying on developers.

NOTE: Please involve Miss Nadia’s team + designer to review site structure, images, and overflow issues, and suggest improvements.

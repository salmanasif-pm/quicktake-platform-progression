## Description

**Problem Statement**

Financial advisors currently face significant **scalability, efficiency, and automation challenges** in managing client portfolios, tax planning, and financial advisory processes. The reliance on **spreadsheets and limited customization of third-party tools** makes the process cumbersome, error-prone, and time-consuming.

The prospect envisions a **cloud-based, automated platform** that integrates **portfolio modeling, tax planning, investment allocation, document management, and collaboration** into a seamless experience for advisors and clients. The goal is to **streamline advisory operations, reduce manual work, improve efficiency, and enhance user experience** while maintaining flexibility for different advisor approaches.

### **Core Challenges**

1. **Manual & Inefficient Data Handling:**
   - Advisors rely on spreadsheets that are prone to human error.
   - Portfolio allocation, tax planning, and financial modeling require manual entry.
   - No structured system to **track changes and maintain version history**.
2. **Fragmented Document Management:**
   - Financial plans, investment strategies, and client documents are stored in multiple locations.
   - No centralized **"Wealth Vault"** to store, organize, and share client data securely.
   - Lack of **document versioning and access control** for sub-advisors (CPAs, attorneys, etc.).
3. **Inefficient Portfolio & Investment Planning:**
   - Current allocation strategy involves **manual spreadsheet calculations**.
   - Advisors need flexibility to **customize investment buckets** but lack automation.
   - Need for **tax-aware investment allocation** to optimize returns.
4. **Limited Scalability & Advisor Productivity:**
   - Advisors handle clients one-by-one with **high time investment per client**.
   - Training new advisors is time-consuming due to lack of structured processes.
   - No standardized workflow automation for client meetings, document uploads, or task tracking.

### **Solution Requirements**

#### **1. Cloud-Based Advisory Platform**

**Role-Based Access & User Management:**

- **Admin:** Full access to all advisor and client data.
- **Advisor:** Access only to assigned clients.
- **Client:** Access only to their personal financial data.
- **Sub-Advisors (CPAs, attorneys, etc.):** Limited access based on advisor-controlled permissions.

**Secure Wealth Vault for Document Management:**

- Centralized storage for financial plans, investment strategies, and legal documents.
- **Version control:** Maintain history of all document updates.
- Access-based controls (e.g., advisor can grant CPA access to tax documents only).
- Automated notifications when a document is uploaded or modified.

**Tracking & Notifications:**

- Advisors receive alerts when clients update information.
- System tracks **percentage of completion** for tasks (e.g., onboarding, document submission, in process etc).

---

#### **2. AI-Driven Financial & Investment Planning**

**Automated Investment Allocation & Customization:**

- Core model with **six investment buckets**, adjustable per advisor.
- Advisors can **override system-generated models** for custom strategies.
- Risk assessment & goal-based tracking for personalized financial planning.

**Integrated Tax Planning & OCR-Based Document Processing:**

- Automated extraction of key financial data from **tax returns, bank statements, and investment reports**.
- AI-powered tax optimization strategies for different portfolio types (IRA, Roth, taxable accounts).
- Pre-filled client financial information for advisors, reducing data entry time.

**Spreadsheet Automation & Dynamic Financial Modeling:**

- Replace existing manual **Excel models** with an interactive digital platform.
- Real-time **scenario modeling** (e.g., moving funds from growth bucket to income bucket).
- Tax-aware investment placement suggestions for advisors.

**Advisor-Customizable Portfolio Models:**

- Base investment models provided, but advisors can **customize allocations**.
- Toggle-based approach: Advisors select **which buckets** they want to use per client.

**Phase 2: AI-powered self-service financial planning for DIY clients.** Like having an LMS in the platform where clients can go through the training self paced and can try to manage their portfolio in a DIY manner but an advisor with specialty areas will still be allocated but this is more of a down the lane goal for them.

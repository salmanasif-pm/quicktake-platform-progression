## Description

## **Problem Statement**

The current recruitment process is highly manual and inefficient, with recruiters spending significant time performing repetitive tasks such as searching for candidates on LinkedIn Recruiter, sending outreach messages, and following up. Despite having a substantial in-house database of 100K candidates (projected to grow to ~600K by November), it remains underutilized due to clunky search capabilities and lack of automation, causing continued over-reliance on LinkedIn Recruiter. This leads to high license costs, inconsistent follow-up, and lost opportunities to engage with qualified candidates already in the system. The goal is to automate sourcing, outreach, and follow-up processes, prioritize internal database usage, and centralize workflows so recruiters can focus on high-value activities such as interviewing and client engagement.

---

## **Requirements (From Prospect Call)**

1. **Candidate Search & Filtering**
   - Ability to search both LinkedIn Recruiter and the internal candidate database.
   - Support for AI / prompt-based filtering (e.g., “Show me active candidates in LA region who we have contacted in the last 6 months”).
   - Automatically identify top 20 candidates for each role.
2. **Candidate Outreach**
   - Automated sending of initial messages to identified candidates.
   - Follow-up messages sent after 3 days if no reply (option for additional touchpoints).
3. **Candidate Activation**
   - Bulk communication to the 600K candidates to determine interest and activity.
   - Categorize/tag candidates as “active” or “inactive” based on responses.
   - Goal: Narrow 600K candidates to an engaged subset (~50K).
4. **Workflow Centralization**
   - Reduce manual recruiter actions (“clicking buttons” on LinkedIn).
   - Centralize tasks so recruiters only engage with candidates who respond positively.
5. **Ownership & Build**
   - Platform must be fully custom-built.
   - All intellectual property belongs to the client.
6. **Database Reliance**
   - Prioritize searching and engaging candidates from the internal database over LinkedIn.
   - LinkedIn to be used with fewer licenses, only as supplemental sourcing.

---

## **Current Manual Workflow vs. Target Automated Workflow**

**Current Manual Workflow:**

1. Recruiter logs into LinkedIn Recruiter.
2. Runs manual searches using filters/keywords from job description.
3. LinkedIn generates candidate list.
4. Recruiter manually sends messages to candidates.
5. Waits for responses (sometimes follows up, sometimes not).
6. If candidate responds, recruiter coordinates screening interview.
7. Selects top candidates to send to client.

**Target Automated Workflow:**

1. Job role entered into system.
2. System searches **internal database** first, then LinkedIn Recruiter (if needed).
3. AI/prompt-based filtering applied to identify best-fit candidates.
4. System selects top 20 candidates automatically.
5. System sends initial personalized outreach message to each candidate.
6. If no reply after 3 days, system sends automated follow-up messages.
7. Candidates who respond positively are flagged as “active” and assigned to recruiters.
8. Recruiter conducts interview with engaged candidates only.

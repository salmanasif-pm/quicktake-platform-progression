## Description

The client’s main challenge is that lab reports are currently being faxed, which is slow, manual, and creates bottlenecks in communication and record-keeping. What she is looking for is a digital solution that streamlines the submission and sharing of lab reports with both patients and client organizations (e.g., multiple laboratories). This includes building a central repository where all reports are stored, accessible via a dashboard, with historical data, search/filter options, and secure access controls. The ultimate goal is to eliminate manual faxing, improve turnaround time, and provide both patients and organizations with an easy, reliable, and trackable way to access lab reports.

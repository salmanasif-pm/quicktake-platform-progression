## Description

**Background**

Seaver Construction currently uses Procore but only utilizes 4 to 5 of its features. The cost scales with project volume, which is becoming unsustainable as they manage 30 to 40 projects but are only inputting about 10 due to cost. They want to own a custom-built alternative so they stop paying recurring licensing fees entirely.

---

**Required Features (MVP)**

1. Submittals
2. RFIs
3. Shop Drawings
4. Daily Reports
5. Specifications Storage

**Future Phase (not in scope now)**

- Cost reporting module (flagged for a later engagement)

---

**Technical Requirements**

- Web plus mobile access via PWA (React preferred, no native app)
- Customizable user permission system (checkbox-based role builder, no pre-built profiles)
- Smart/controlled notifications (user selects what they receive)
- Must support 30 to 40 concurrent projects and multiple business verticals

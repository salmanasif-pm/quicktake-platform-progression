## Part 1: Website Revamp Requirements

### **Goal:**

Modernize all three websites with consistent branding, better UX/UI, improved mobile performance, and prep for future AI/chatbot integrations.

### **Proposed Platform:**

Migrate **all three** websites to **WordPress**, to unify the tech stack and avoid GoDaddy platform limitations.

### **Functional Requirements**

- Clean, professional, mobile-optimized design
- Fast loading, SEO-optimized
- Easy-to-manage backend for non-technical users
- Clear CTAs (e.g. Order Now, Contact Us, Subscribe)
- Embedded chatbot widget on each site (see Chatbot section below)
- Secure contact forms & email integration
- Blog or updates/news section (optional)

### **Design Preferences**

- Friendly, minimal, and modern (especially for My Green Lunch, targeting parents and schools)
- Kid-centric elements for My Green Lunch (think Salesforce-style cartoons as reference)
- Premium, pet-lifestyle vibe for Buddy’s Batch
- Clean & wholesome branding for My Hero Kitchen

### **Additional Needs**

- Remove dependency on GoDaddy (migrate hosting/email if needed)
- Avoid excessive plugin use (lean and secure WordPress builds)
- Future extensibility for custom features (e.g. order management)

---

## Part 2: AI Chatbot Requirements

### **Goal:**

Implement an AI-powered chatbot on all three sites to:

- Answer FAQs
- Guide customers to the right information
- Reduce manual support requests
- Escalate to human support if needed

### **Functional Requirements**

- Widget embedded on all three websites
- Supports text-based interaction
- Covers distinct FAQ sets for:
  - My Green Lunch (school orders, menu, allergies, policies, etc.)
  - Buddy’s Batch (dog food prep, delivery, nutrition, transition help)
  - My Hero Kitchen (corporate ordering, EasyCater inquiries, donation model, salad ingredients)
- Can pull from structured content (e.g. provided docs or FAQs)
- Includes fallback escalation option (“Talk to a real person”)
- Support for future multi-language capability (optional)
